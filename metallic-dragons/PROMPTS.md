## Shared direction

Use case: logo-brand. Asset type: family-friendly classic-fantasy tabletop game marker artwork for a flush two-colour 25 mm FDM token. Create one original adult dragon head-and-shoulders emblem matching a bold compact token collection as exactly one connected near-square solid-black silhouette. Front-facing dragon head, two large eye openings, two broad nostril openings, one thick closed-mouth gap, and only large negative spaces. Designed around 18 mm for a 0.4 mm FDM nozzle and direct tracing without dilation, erosion, cleanup, hole filling, island removal, or line thickening. Every horn, crest, bridge, and opening must be about 6% of artwork width or wider and connected. Pure black on opaque white with hard edges. No grayscale, transparency, shading, colour, token circle, border, text, wings, body, scenery, elemental effects, injury imagery, open mouth, teeth, tiny scales, thin spikes, narrow lines, isolated islands, floating pieces, or second creature.
