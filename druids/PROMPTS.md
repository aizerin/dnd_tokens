# Druids — Image generation prompts

Generated with the built-in ImageGen tool.

## Shared direction

Use case: logo-brand. Asset type: family-friendly classic-fantasy adventurer or NPC marker artwork for a flush two-colour 25 mm FDM token. Create one original humanoid class emblem matching a bold compact token collection as exactly one front-facing head-and-shoulders figure in one connected near-square solid-black silhouette, with two large eye openings and only large negative spaces. Designed around 18 mm for a 0.4 mm FDM nozzle and direct tracing without dilation, erosion, cleanup, hole filling, island removal, or line thickening. Every branch, staff, hand shape, bridge, and opening must be about 6% of artwork width or wider and fused to the main silhouette. Pure black on opaque white with hard edges. No grayscale, transparency, shading, colour, token circle, border, text, scenery, injury imagery, tiny leaves, thin hair, narrow fingers, isolated islands, floating pieces, spell particles, animal companion, or second character.

## Creature requests

- **Herbalist Druid:** Calm hooded druid with a broad leaf collar containing exactly four large leaf openings and one thick branch staff fused to a shoulder with one large loop. No loose herbs.
- **Shapeshifter Druid:** Hooded druid wearing a broad bear-inspired mask fused directly into the hood and shoulders, two large eye openings and exactly four broad fur collar points. No separate animal.
- **Forest Druid:** Hooded forest guardian with a broad antler crown made of exactly four thick connected branches, high leaf collar and one short thick staff fused to a shoulder.
- **Archdruid:** Regal robed druid with a broad six-lobed living-wood crown fused into a huge leaf collar and one thick spiral branch staff fused to a shoulder with exactly two large openings.
