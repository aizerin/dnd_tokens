# Monstrosities II — Image generation prompts

Generated with the built-in ImageGen tool.

## Shared direction

Use case: logo-brand. Asset type: family-friendly classic-fantasy tabletop game marker artwork for a flush two-colour 25 mm FDM token. Create one original creature emblem matching a bold compact token collection as exactly one connected near-square solid-black silhouette with only large negative spaces. Designed around 18 mm for a 0.4 mm FDM nozzle and direct tracing without dilation, erosion, cleanup, hole filling, island removal, or line thickening. Every limb, wing, horn, bridge, and opening must be about 6% of artwork width or wider and connected. Pure black on opaque white with hard edges. No grayscale, transparency, shading, colour, token circle, border, text, scenery, injury imagery, tiny texture, thin spikes, narrow lines, isolated islands, floating pieces, or second creature.

## Creature requests

- **Cockatrice:** One front-facing rooster-dragon head-and-shoulders emblem: broad beak, two large eyes, exactly three blunt comb points, two thick horn nubs and two broad folded wings fused to the shoulders.
- **Harpy:** One front-facing wild humanoid-bird head and shoulders: stern face, two large eyes, broad feather crown and two broad folded wings fused behind the shoulders, each with one large opening.
- **Centaur:** One compact side-profile centaur emblem: humanoid torso fused directly into a broad horse body, exactly four short fused legs, one thick bow fused along the back and one large body opening.
- **Hippogriff:** One compact side-profile eagle-horse creature: broad hooked beak, one large eye, two folded wings fused to the body, exactly four short fused legs and one large wing opening.
