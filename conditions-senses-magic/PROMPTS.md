## Shared direction

Use case: logo-brand. Asset type: family-friendly D&D condition marker artwork for a flush two-colour 25 mm FDM token. One original, bold, compact status emblem as a single near-square solid-black silhouette with only large negative spaces. Instantly readable at 18 mm, suitable for a 0.4 mm FDM nozzle and direct tracing without dilation, erosion, cleanup, hole filling, island removal or line thickening. Every stroke, bridge and opening is broad, approximately 7% of artwork width or wider. Pure black on opaque white with hard edges. No grayscale, transparency, shading, colour, token circle, outer border, text, letters, numbers, scenery, blood, injury detail, tiny particles, thin lines, isolated islands or floating pieces.

- **Concentration:** one front-facing simplified head silhouette fused to a huge faceted diamond above the brow, with two large eye openings and exactly three large facet openings inside the diamond. No rays, sparkles or separate shapes.
