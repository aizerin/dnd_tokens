# Paladins — Image generation prompts

Generated with the built-in ImageGen tool.

## Shared direction

Use case: logo-brand. Asset type: family-friendly classic-fantasy adventurer or NPC marker artwork for a flush two-colour 25 mm FDM token. Create one original humanoid class emblem matching a bold compact token collection as exactly one front-facing head-and-shoulders figure in one connected near-square solid-black silhouette, with two large eye openings and only large negative spaces. Designed around 18 mm for a 0.4 mm FDM nozzle and direct tracing without dilation, erosion, cleanup, hole filling, island removal, or line thickening. Every weapon, shield, crest, bridge, and opening must be about 6% of artwork width or wider and fused to the main silhouette. Pure black on opaque white with hard edges. No grayscale, transparency, shading, colour, token circle, border, text, scenery, injury imagery, tiny jewellery, thin hair, narrow blades, isolated islands, floating pieces, spell particles, animal companion, or second character.

## Creature requests

- **Guardian Paladin:** Armored paladin with a broad closed helmet, one huge shield fused behind both shoulders containing one large cross-shaped opening and no weapon.
- **Crusader Paladin:** Armored paladin with a tall blunt crest, one short thick warhammer fused behind one shoulder and one broad kite shield fused behind the other with a diamond opening.
- **Oathkeeper Paladin:** Open-faced paladin with a broad crown-like helmet, high cloak collar and one thick sword fused vertically behind the head with one large circular pommel opening.
- **Avenger Paladin:** Angular armored paladin with a wing-shaped helmet crest fused to the shoulders and one huge broad two-handed sword fused diagonally behind both shoulders.
