# Sorcerers — Image generation prompts

Generated with the built-in ImageGen tool.

## Shared direction

Use case: logo-brand. Asset type: family-friendly classic-fantasy adventurer or NPC marker artwork for a flush two-colour 25 mm FDM token. Create one original humanoid spellcaster class emblem matching a bold compact token collection as exactly one front-facing head-and-shoulders figure in one connected near-square solid-black silhouette, with two large eye openings and only large negative spaces. Designed around 18 mm for a 0.4 mm FDM nozzle and direct tracing without dilation, erosion, cleanup, hole filling, island removal, or line thickening. Every horn, crest, staff, bridge, and opening must be about 6% of artwork width or wider and fused to the main silhouette. Pure black on opaque white with hard edges. No grayscale, transparency, shading, colour, token circle, border, text, scenery, injury imagery, tiny jewellery, thin hair, narrow lines, isolated islands, floating pieces, spell particles, flames, lightning bolts, or second character.

## Creature requests

- **Draconic Sorcerer:** Regal spellcaster with exactly two thick backward horn-like crown points, broad scale-inspired cheek plates, high collar and one thick dragon-head staff fused to a shoulder with one large opening.
- **Wild Magic Sorcerer:** Expressive spellcaster with an asymmetric broad three-lobed hood, high curled collar and one thick spiral staff fused to a shoulder with exactly three large openings. No loose effects.
- **Storm Sorcerer:** Stern spellcaster with a broad swept crest shaped like exactly four connected cloud lobes, high angular collar and one thick fork-topped staff fused to a shoulder with one large opening.
- **Shadow Sorcerer:** Deep-hooded spellcaster with two large eye openings, broad crescent collar and one thick crescent-topped staff fused to a shoulder with exactly one large opening.
