# Plant Creatures — Image generation prompts

Generated with the built-in ImageGen tool.

## Shared direction

Use case: logo-brand. Asset type: family-friendly classic-fantasy tabletop game marker artwork for a flush two-colour 25 mm FDM token. Create one original creature emblem matching a bold compact token collection as exactly one connected near-square solid-black silhouette with only large negative spaces. Designed around 18 mm for a 0.4 mm FDM nozzle and direct tracing without dilation, erosion, cleanup, hole filling, island removal, or line thickening. Every branch, limb, wing, bridge, and opening must be about 6% of artwork width or wider and connected. Pure black on opaque white with hard edges. No grayscale, transparency, shading, colour, token circle, border, text, scenery, injury imagery, tiny texture, thin twigs, narrow lines, isolated islands, floating pieces, or second creature.

## Creature requests

- **Twig Blight:** One compact full-body twig humanoid with a large head, two thick branch arms fused back into the torso, two short fused legs, two large eyes and exactly four broad thorn points.
- **Needle Blight:** One compact full-body conifer humanoid with a triangular crown, two thick arms fused to the sides, two short legs, two eyes and exactly six broad needle clusters. No loose needles.
- **Vine Blight:** One front-facing plant humanoid torso made from exactly four thick vines that loop and reconnect, two eye openings and two broad leaf-shaped shoulders. No loose tendrils.
- **Awakened Tree:** One front-facing ancient tree face and trunk: huge broad crown fused to shoulders, two large eye openings, one mouth gap, exactly four thick branch lobes and three root lobes.
