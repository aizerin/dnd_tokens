# Swarms & Vermin — Image generation prompts

Generated with the built-in ImageGen tool.

## Shared direction

Use case: logo-brand. Asset type: family-friendly classic-fantasy tabletop game marker artwork for a flush two-colour 25 mm FDM token. Create one original creature emblem matching a bold compact token collection as exactly one connected near-square solid-black silhouette with only large negative spaces. Designed around 18 mm for a 0.4 mm FDM nozzle and direct tracing without dilation, erosion, cleanup, hole filling, island removal, or line thickening. Every branch, limb, wing, bridge, and opening must be about 6% of artwork width or wider and connected. Pure black on opaque white with hard edges. No grayscale, transparency, shading, colour, token circle, border, text, scenery, injury imagery, tiny texture, thin twigs, narrow lines, isolated islands, floating pieces, or second creature.

## Creature requests

- **Swarm of Rats:** One connected circular emblem of exactly three overlapping rat silhouettes fused together by bodies and tails, with three broad head shapes and one thick outer tail arc. No separate rats.
- **Swarm of Bats:** One connected emblem of exactly three overlapping bat silhouettes fused wing-to-wing around a central body, with six broad wing openings total. No separate bats.
- **Swarm of Insects:** One connected emblem of exactly five large beetle-like bodies fused into a honeycomb cluster by thick legs, with only six large openings. No tiny insects.
- **Swarm of Ravens:** One connected emblem of exactly three broad raven silhouettes fused wing-to-wing in a spiral, with three beaks and six large wing openings. No separate birds.
