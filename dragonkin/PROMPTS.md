# Dragonkin — Image generation prompts

Generated with the built-in ImageGen tool.

## Shared direction

Use case: logo-brand. Asset type: family-friendly classic-fantasy tabletop game marker artwork for a flush two-colour 25 mm FDM token. Create one original creature emblem matching a bold compact token collection as exactly one connected near-square solid-black silhouette with only large negative spaces. Designed around 18 mm for a 0.4 mm FDM nozzle and direct tracing without dilation, erosion, cleanup, hole filling, island removal, or line thickening. Every limb, wing, tail, bridge, and opening must be about 6% of artwork width or wider and connected. Pure black on opaque white with hard edges. No grayscale, transparency, shading, colour, token circle, border, text, scenery, injury imagery, tiny feathers or scales, thin spikes, narrow lines, isolated islands, floating pieces, or second creature.

## Creature requests

- **Black Dragon Wyrmling:** One front-facing young dragon head and shoulders with a long narrow snout, two large eyes, two thick forward-curving horns, broad cheek fins and exactly four large facial openings. Closed mouth.
- **Pseudodragon:** One front-facing tiny dragon head and shoulders with huge rounded ears, two large eyes, two broad folded wings fused behind and a thick curled tail reconnected to one wing.
- **Faerie Dragon:** One front-facing playful small dragon head and shoulders with broad butterfly-like wings fused behind, each with exactly two large openings, two eyes and two short horn nubs. No sparkles.
- **Wyvern:** One front-facing angular dragon head and shoulders with two broad bat wings fused behind, two large eyes, two thick backward horns and a thick stinger tail arch reconnected behind one wing.
