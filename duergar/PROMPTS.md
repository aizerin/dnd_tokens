# Duergar — Image generation prompts

Generated with the built-in ImageGen tool.

## Shared direction

Use case: logo-brand. Family-friendly classic-fantasy tabletop marker artwork for a flush two-colour 25 mm FDM token. Create one original Duergar emblem as one front-facing underground dwarf head-and-shoulders figure in a connected near-square solid-black silhouette, with a broad square face, heavy beard, two large eye openings, and only large negative spaces. Designed around 18 mm for a 0.4 mm FDM nozzle and direct tracing without morphology or cleanup. Every feature must be about 5% of artwork width or wider and connected to the silhouette. Pure black on opaque white with hard edges. No grayscale, transparency, shading, colour, token circle, border, text, scenery, injury imagery, tiny runes, thin hair, narrow lines, isolated islands, floating pieces, or second creature.

## Creature requests

- **Duergar Warrior:** Heavy hornless iron helmet, two broad cheek plates, exactly three thick beard sections, and one short thick war pick fused behind one shoulder.
- **Duergar Crossbowman:** Low angular helmet, square braided beard represented by exactly three broad sections, and one compact thick crossbow fused horizontally behind the shoulders. No loose bolt.
- **Duergar Overseer:** Tall blocky helmet, heavy moustache, exactly four broad beard plates, and one thick hooked hammer fused to one shoulder. Stern closed mouth.
- **Duergar Runesmith:** Broad forge mask lifted above the eyes, exactly three large beard sections, and one thick square-headed smith hammer fused to one shoulder. One large diamond opening in the hammer head; no sparks or tiny runes.
