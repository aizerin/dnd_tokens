# Yuan-ti — Image generation prompts

Generated with the built-in ImageGen tool.

## Shared direction

Use case: logo-brand. Asset type: family-friendly classic-fantasy tabletop game marker artwork for a flush two-colour 25 mm FDM token. Designed around 18 mm for a 0.4 mm FDM nozzle and direct tracing without dilation, erosion, cleanup, hole filling, island removal, or line thickening. Exactly one front-facing head-and-shoulders figure as one connected near-square solid-black silhouette with two large eye openings and only large negative spaces. Every feature, weapon, bridge, and opening must be about 5% of artwork width or wider and connected. Pure black on opaque white with hard edges. No grayscale, transparency, shading, colour, token circle, border, text, scenery, injury imagery, tiny texture, thin spikes, narrow lines, isolated islands, floating pieces, or second creature. Create one original Yuan-ti emblem matching a bold compact token collection. Subject anatomy: serpent humanoid with a smooth snake face, broad hood or collar and thick shoulders.

## Creature requests

- **Yuan-ti Pureblood:** One very simple front-facing serpent-human portrait: mostly human oval face, two large eye openings, broad nose opening, one thick closed-mouth gap, two smooth snake cheek plates, and a small rounded cobra hood fused directly into the shoulders. Exactly six negative spaces; fewer than twelve outer contour turns; every bridge and opening at least 7% of artwork width. No hair, jewellery, crown, weapon, staff, tail, scales, texture, or extra ornament.
- **Yuan-ti Archer:** Small cobra hood and one thick curved bow fused behind the shoulders with one broad arrow fused to it.
- **Yuan-ti Malison:** Large cobra head, huge hood with exactly two large openings and exactly three broad neck bands. No weapon.
- **Yuan-ti Priest:** Tall cobra crown, broad hood and one thick serpent-topped staff fused to a shoulder, forming one large opening.
