# Constructs — Image Generation Prompts

Built-in ImageGen was used separately for every token.

## Shared prompt

> Use case: logo-brand. One artificial magical creature or animated object as a compact connected solid-black emblem for a flush two-colour 25 mm FDM token. Designed around 18 mm for a 0.4 mm nozzle and direct tracing without dilation, erosion, cleanup, hole filling, island removal or thickening. Pure black on opaque white; no shading, colour, border, text, thin joints, tiny rivets, repeated gear teeth or floating pieces.

## Role blocks

- **Animated Armor:** Empty closed plate armour with broad eye slits and shoulder plates.
- **Flying Sword:** Oversized sword connected to a broad two-opening magic loop.
- **Stone Golem:** Blocky head and shoulders with three short broad cracks.
- **Clockwork Guardian:** Mechanical humanoid with round eyes and one six-tooth chest gear.
