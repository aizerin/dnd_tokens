## Shared direction

Designed around 18 mm for a 0.4 mm FDM nozzle and direct tracing without dilation, erosion, cleanup, hole filling, island removal, or line thickening. No grayscale, transparency, shading, colour, token circle, border, text, scenery, injury imagery, tiny texture, thin spikes, narrow lines, isolated islands, floating pieces, or second creature. Create one original Sahuagin emblem matching a bold compact token collection. Subject anatomy: shark-like sea humanoid with a long angular snout, tall dorsal crest and thick shoulders.
