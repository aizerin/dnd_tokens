# Magical Traps — Image generation prompts

Generated with the built-in ImageGen tool.

## Shared direction

Use case: logo-brand. Asset type: family-friendly classic-fantasy trap or environmental hazard marker artwork for a flush two-colour 25 mm FDM token. Create one original non-graphic hazard emblem matching a bold compact token collection as exactly one connected near-square solid-black silhouette with only large negative spaces. Use a clear top-down or front diagram-like view. Designed around 18 mm for a 0.4 mm FDM nozzle and direct tracing without dilation, erosion, cleanup, hole filling, island removal or line thickening. Every rune stroke, cloud band, rock, bridge and opening must be about 7% of artwork width or wider and fused into one symbol. Pure black on opaque white with hard edges. No grayscale, transparency, shading, colour, token circle outside the hazard, border, text, scenery, injury imagery, blood, tiny runes, particles, thin cracks, narrow lines, isolated islands, floating pieces, people or creatures.

## Creature requests

- **Fire Rune:** One top-down magical rune: thick circular outer band connected to exactly five broad rounded flame lobes and one huge central diamond opening, creating exactly four large inner gaps. No separate flames.
- **Frost Rune:** One top-down magical rune: thick hexagonal outer band connected to exactly six broad blunt crystal arms and one huge central hexagon opening. No snowflakes or loose shards.
- **Lightning Rune:** One top-down magical rune: thick circular outer band connected to exactly four huge angular zigzag arms meeting around one large central diamond opening. No separate bolts.
- **Binding Rune:** One top-down magical rune: thick square outer band connected to exactly four huge interlocking chain-like loops, with one large central circular opening and four large corner gaps. No thin chains.
