# Undead II — Image generation prompts

## Shared direction

Use case: logo-brand. Asset type: family-friendly classic-fantasy tabletop game marker artwork for a flush two-colour 25 mm FDM token. Create one original family-friendly fantasy creature emblem matching a bold compact token collection as exactly one connected near-square solid-black silhouette with only large negative spaces and no graphic content. Designed around 18 mm for a 0.4 mm FDM nozzle and direct tracing without dilation, erosion, cleanup, hole filling, island removal, or line thickening. Every horn, limb, wing, bridge, and opening must be about 6% of artwork width or wider and connected. Pure black on opaque white with hard edges. No grayscale, transparency, shading, colour, token circle, border, text, scenery, injury imagery, exposed organs, gore, tiny texture, thin spikes, narrow lines, isolated islands, floating pieces, or second creature.

- **Crawling Claw:** One stylized magical skeletal hand emblem, palm front-facing, exactly five thick fingers fused by a broad palm and one large central palm opening. No severed edge or injury detail.
- **Shadow:** One hooded humanoid shadow head-and-shoulders silhouette with two large eye openings, exactly four broad cloak folds and arms fused back into the shoulders. No face details.
- **Specter:** One front-facing ghostly hood and torso with two large eyes, one broad mouth gap, exactly three wide body openings and a rounded tail base fused back into the torso.
- **Wraith:** One front-facing crowned hooded apparition with two large eyes, exactly four broad crown points, high collar and exactly three wide robe openings. No weapon.
