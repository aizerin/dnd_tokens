# Dwarves — Image Generation Prompts

The source artwork was created with the built-in image generator. Each token was generated separately with the shared prompt and one role block.

## Shared prompt

> Use case: logo-brand.
>
> Asset type: source artwork for a flush two-colour 25 mm FDM-printed tabletop token.
>
> Collection style: create one completely new original classic-fantasy DWARF pictogram matching a bold compact head-and-shoulders tabletop-token collection. The dwarf must be unmistakably short, broad and sturdy through a very wide head, heavy shoulders, large nose and one oversized solid beard mass. Use a strong graphic silhouette, readable face, role-defining headgear, and exactly one short piece of equipment integrated behind one shoulder. This is not realistic portrait art, not line art, not a heraldic crest and not a comic mascot.
>
> Composition: exactly one front-facing dwarf shown as a compact near-square head-and-shoulders emblem. Use one dominant solid-black silhouette. Keep equipment short, very thick, close to the shoulder, and firmly fused into the bust. Leave a generous white margin around the complete glyph.
>
> Face and beard openings: exactly two large simple eye openings with no pupils, one broad simple nose opening, and one short thick neutral mouth gap within or immediately above the beard. The mouth must be a plain closed slit, not lips, not a smile, not a frown, not an open mouth, and no teeth. The beard is one solid broad mass with at most three large internal gaps and at most four oversized blunt outer points. Maximum eight white openings total.
>
> Manufacturing constraints: final glyph is approximately 18 mm wide on a 25 mm token and printed with a 0.4 mm FDM nozzle. It will be traced directly without dilation, erosion, cleanup, hole filling, island removal or line thickening. Every black bridge, equipment connector and white opening must be about 5% of the artwork width or wider. No thin helmet lines, chainmail texture, narrow weapon shafts, fragile points, tiny rivets, individual beard hairs, narrow braids, cloth folds, runes, isolated islands or floating pieces.
>
> Output: pure solid black artwork on a pure opaque white background with hard crisp edges. No grayscale, transparency, shading, gradients, texture, shadow, colour, token circle, rim, border, frame, text, letters or numbers. No shield, animal, second character or unrequested equipment.

## Dwarf Warrior role block

> Primary request: DWARF WARRIOR. Show a stern professional dwarf infantryman wearing one broad hornless angular helmet with thick cheek guards and a heavy armour collar. Give him one large squared beard ending in exactly three broad blunt points. Add one short very thick single-bladed battleaxe behind one shoulder, fused into the helmet or collar, with an oversized crescent axe head and a blunt short handle. No hood, crown, plume, bow, crossbow, sword, pickaxe, hammer or decorative symbols.

## Dwarf Crossbowman role block

> Primary request: DWARF CROSSBOWMAN. Show a focused dwarf marksman wearing one low rounded helmet over a simple close-fitting hood and a broad padded collar. Give him one wide beard divided by exactly two large vertical gaps into three thick blunt sections. Add one short oversized crossbow bolt diagonally behind one shoulder, with a very thick shaft, one broad triangular point and one simplified solid fletching block, firmly fused into the bust. No axe, hammer, pickaxe, sword, shield, actual crossbow, plume or decorative symbols.

## Dwarf Miner role block

> Primary request: DWARF MINER. Show a rugged working dwarf wearing one simple low mining cap with a single large rectangular lamp fused into the front of the cap; the lamp is a solid black block with no tiny interior detail. Give him one broad rounded beard with exactly two large blunt lower points. Add one short very thick pickaxe behind one shoulder, fused into the cap or collar, with a broad curved head and a blunt short handle. Use a tired but neutral expression. No helmet horns, armour, axe, battle hammer, sword, backpack, lantern, gems or decorative symbols.

## Dwarf Runesmith role block

> Primary request: DWARF RUNESMITH. Show an older master dwarf craftsperson wearing one thick plain metal headband and one massive high leather apron collar. Give him a huge rectangular beard with a broad flat bottom and exactly one large central gap. Add one short very thick smithing hammer behind one shoulder, fused into the headband or collar, with an oversized solid rectangular hammer head and a blunt short handle. Use a wise severe neutral expression. No written rune, letters, crown, helmet horns, axe, pickaxe, sword, anvil, flame, sparks or decorative symbols.
