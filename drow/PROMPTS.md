## Shared direction

Use case: logo-brand. Family-friendly classic-fantasy tabletop marker artwork for a flush two-colour 25 mm FDM token. Create one original Drow emblem as exactly one front-facing dark-elf head-and-shoulders figure in one connected near-square solid-black silhouette, with long pointed ears, two large eye openings, and only a few broad negative spaces. Designed around 18 mm for a 0.4 mm FDM nozzle and direct tracing without dilation, erosion, cleanup, hole filling, island removal, or line thickening. Every feature must be about 5% of artwork width or wider and connected to the main silhouette. Pure black on opaque white with hard edges. No grayscale, transparency, shading, colour, token circle, border, text, scenery, injury imagery, tiny jewellery, thin hair strands, narrow lines, isolated islands, floating pieces, or second creature.
