# Fey II — Image generation prompts

Generated with the built-in ImageGen tool.

## Shared direction

Use case: logo-brand. Asset type: family-friendly classic-fantasy tabletop game marker artwork for a flush two-colour 25 mm FDM token. Create one original creature emblem matching a bold compact token collection as exactly one connected near-square solid-black silhouette with only large negative spaces. Designed around 18 mm for a 0.4 mm FDM nozzle and direct tracing without dilation, erosion, cleanup, hole filling, island removal, or line thickening. Every limb, wing, horn, bridge, and opening must be about 6% of artwork width or wider and connected. Pure black on opaque white with hard edges. No grayscale, transparency, shading, colour, token circle, border, text, scenery, injury imagery, tiny texture, thin spikes, narrow lines, isolated islands, floating pieces, or second creature.

## Creature requests

- **Sprite:** One front-facing tiny fairy head and shoulders with pointed ears and exactly two broad leaf-shaped wings fused behind, each containing one large opening. No weapon.
- **Blink Dog:** One front-facing magical hound head and shoulders with broad upright ears, two large eyes, thick muzzle and one broad spiral collar opening. No sparkles.
- **Darkling:** One hooded fey head and shoulders with pointed ears, two large eye openings, broad face mask and one short thick dagger fused behind one shoulder. Closed mouth.
- **Sea Hag:** One front-facing old aquatic fey head and shoulders with broad tangled seaweed hair represented by exactly six thick locks, two large eyes, wide nose and one closed mouth gap. No gore.
