## Shared direction

Use case: logo-brand. Asset type: family-friendly classic-fantasy tabletop game marker artwork for a flush two-colour 25 mm FDM token. Designed around 18 mm for a 0.4 mm FDM nozzle and direct tracing without dilation, erosion, cleanup, hole filling, island removal, or line thickening. Exactly one front-facing head-and-shoulders figure as one connected near-square solid-black silhouette with two large eye openings and only large negative spaces. No grayscale, transparency, shading, colour, token circle, border, text, scenery, injury imagery, tiny texture, thin spikes, narrow lines, isolated islands, floating pieces, or second creature. Create one original Kuo-toa emblem matching a bold compact token collection. Subject anatomy: deep-sea fish humanoid with a round fish mouth, side fins and thick neck.
