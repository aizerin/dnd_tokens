# Fiends II — Image generation prompts

Generated with the built-in ImageGen tool.

## Shared direction

Use case: logo-brand. Asset type: family-friendly classic-fantasy tabletop game marker artwork for a flush two-colour 25 mm FDM token. Create one original family-friendly fantasy creature emblem matching a bold compact token collection as exactly one connected near-square solid-black silhouette with only large negative spaces and no graphic content. Designed around 18 mm for a 0.4 mm FDM nozzle and direct tracing without dilation, erosion, cleanup, hole filling, island removal, or line thickening. Every horn, limb, wing, bridge, and opening must be about 6% of artwork width or wider and connected. Pure black on opaque white with hard edges. No grayscale, transparency, shading, colour, token circle, border, text, scenery, injury imagery, exposed organs, gore, tiny texture, thin spikes, narrow lines, isolated islands, floating pieces, or second creature.

## Creature requests

- **Dretch:** One squat full-body lesser fiend with a huge rounded head, two short blunt horns, two large eyes, one broad mouth gap, stubby arms fused to the body and short fused feet. No weapon.
- **Manes:** One compact full-body amorphous lesser fiend with a bald rounded head, two large eyes, one thick mouth gap, exactly three body-fold openings and two short fused arms. No horns.
- **Spined Devil:** One front-facing winged fiend head and shoulders with two broad bat wings fused behind, two eyes, short snout and exactly six broad blunt spine points. Thick curled tail reconnects to a wing.
- **Bearded Devil:** One front-facing armored fiend head and shoulders with two thick backward horns, two eyes, exactly five broad beard tendrils fused into a single collar and one short thick glaive fused behind a shoulder.
