# Rogues — Image generation prompts

Generated with the built-in ImageGen tool.

## Shared direction

Use case: logo-brand. Asset type: family-friendly classic-fantasy adventurer or NPC marker artwork for a flush two-colour 25 mm FDM token. Create one original humanoid class emblem matching a bold compact token collection as exactly one front-facing head-and-shoulders figure in one connected near-square solid-black silhouette, with two large eye openings and only large negative spaces. Designed around 18 mm for a 0.4 mm FDM nozzle and direct tracing without dilation, erosion, cleanup, hole filling, island removal, or line thickening. Every hat, weapon, staff, bridge, and opening must be about 6% of artwork width or wider and fused to the silhouette. Pure black on opaque white with hard edges. No grayscale, transparency, shading, colour, token circle, border, text, scenery, injury imagery, tiny jewellery, thin hair, narrow blades, isolated islands, floating pieces, spell particles, or second character.

## Creature requests

- **Thief:** Hooded thief with broad face mask, two large eyes, high cloak collar and one short thick lockpick-like hook fused to a shoulder. No coins or bag.
- **Assassin:** Deep-hooded masked assassin with two large eye openings, broad angular collar and one short thick dagger fused diagonally behind one shoulder. Closed mouth.
- **Scout:** Lightly hooded scout with two large eyes, broad scarf, one compact thick shortbow fused behind the shoulders and one broad arrow fused to it.
- **Swashbuckler:** Confident adventurer with a broad feathered hat where the feather reconnects to the brim, open face, high collar and one short thick curved sword fused behind a shoulder.
