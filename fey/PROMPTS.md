# Fey — Image Generation Prompts

Built-in ImageGen was used separately for every token.

## Shared prompt

> Use case: logo-brand. One original front-facing fey creature as a connected compact solid-black emblem for a flush two-colour 25 mm FDM token. Designed around 18 mm for a 0.4 mm nozzle and direct tracing without dilation, erosion, cleanup, hole filling, island removal or thickening. Wings, horns and branches are broad and connected. Pure black on opaque white; no shading, colour, border, text, thin vines, fine hair, tiny leaves or floating pieces.

## Role blocks

- **Pixie:** Youthful face, pointed ears and two broad connected butterfly wings.
- **Dryad:** Bark-haired tree spirit with reconnecting branches and broad leaves.
- **Satyr:** Bearded face, pointed ears, curled horns and fur collar.
- **Redcap:** Drooping cap, pointed ears, ragged collar and connected broad sickle.
