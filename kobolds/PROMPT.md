# New Artwork Prompt

The Kobold Warrior, Slinger, and Trapper come from the previously approved set. The Kobold Shaman was newly created with the built-in image generator. The existing kobolds were used only as references for style and faction consistency.

> Use case: logo-brand. Asset type: source artwork for a flush two-colour 25 mm FDM-printed tabletop token. Input images: Images 1, 2, and 3 are style and creature-family references only. Create a completely new original kobold shaman glyph that clearly belongs to the same reptilian kobold faction. Do not copy a reference pose or its equipment. Primary request: exactly one centered black-and-white pictogram of a reptilian kobold shaman, shown as a compact front-facing head-and-shoulders emblem. Give it a long angular reptilian snout, swept-back horns, a broad primitive bone crown, and one thick crooked ritual staff fused tightly to one shoulder. The staff and crown must be visually dominant enough to distinguish the shaman from a warrior, slinger, and trapper. Print constraints: the final artwork will be approximately 18 mm wide on a 25 mm token and printed with a 0.4 mm FDM nozzle. There will be no later dilation, erosion, stroke thickening, or cleanup. Make every black stroke, connector, facial feature, and important white gap broad, approximately 5% of the artwork width or more. Use thick rounded graphic shapes, large clear cutouts, generous padding, and a visually connected compact composition. Color and background: pure solid black artwork on a pure white background, crisp hard edges. Avoid: enclosing circle, token rim, border, text, letters, numbers, gray, color, gradients, shadows, texture, perspective, 3D rendering, fine linework, thin details, tiny holes, isolated floating fragments, extra objects, weapons other than the ritual staff.

The technical conversion only traces the artwork boundary. It does not use dilation, erosion, or artwork thickening.
