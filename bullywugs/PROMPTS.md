Designed around 18 mm for a 0.4 mm FDM nozzle and direct tracing without dilation, erosion, cleanup, hole filling, island removal, or line thickening. No grayscale, transparency, shading, colour, token circle, border, text, scenery, injury imagery, tiny texture, thin spikes, narrow lines, isolated islands, floating pieces, or second creature. Create one original Bullywugs emblem matching a bold compact token collection. Subject anatomy: frog humanoid with a huge rounded mouth, wide-set eyes and thick shoulders.
