# Animals Artwork Prompts

All nine images were created with the built-in image generator. The Wolf established the simplified graphic language for this category and became the primary style reference for the remaining animals.

## Wolf

> Use case: logo-brand. Asset type: source artwork for a flush two-colour 25 mm FDM-printed tabletop token. Create one completely new original WOLF animal pictogram. The input image is only a broad boldness reference; simplify much more than it.
>
> Subject: exactly one front-facing natural grey-wolf head and short upper neck, clearly a real quadruped animal rather than a werewolf. Use two upright thick triangular ears with two single large inner-ear openings, one broad forehead, exactly two large solid almond-shaped eyes with NO pupils and NO highlights, one long broad muzzle, and one single solid nose with NO nostril holes. Use only four large cheek-fur points per side at most. Closed mouth represented by one broad simple gap. No visible teeth. No humanoid shoulders, armour, mane, weapon, collar, scars, or supernatural expression.
>
> FDM constraint: final artwork is approximately 18 mm wide, printed with a 0.4 mm nozzle, and traced directly with no later dilation, erosion, or cleanup. Every black bridge and every white opening must be very broad, at least about 5% of the artwork width. Use a minimal connected silhouette and only a handful of large openings. Absolutely no tiny circles, pupil dots, eye highlights, nostril dots, whiskers, hair strands, narrow eye rings, thin lips, small teeth, tiny fur notches, isolated islands, or floating fragments.
>
> Centered compact near-square logo pictogram. Pure solid black artwork on a pure white background with hard crisp edges. No grayscale, shading, gradients, texture, shadows, colour, border, enclosing circle, frame, lettering, caption, numbers, or text.

## Shared prompt for the remaining animals

> Use case: logo-brand. Asset type: source artwork for a flush two-colour 25 mm FDM-printed tabletop token. Input image: the supplied Wolf is the primary collection style and simplification reference. Create one completely new original animal glyph, not a copy of the wolf's anatomy.
>
> Manufacturing constraint: final artwork is approximately 18 mm wide on a 25 mm token and printed with a 0.4 mm FDM nozzle. It will be converted directly to SVG with no later dilation, erosion, cleanup, hole filling, island removal, or line thickening. Use a minimal sturdy connected silhouette and only a handful of very large openings. Every black bridge and every white gap must be at least about 5% of the artwork width. Absolutely no pupil dots, eye highlights, nostril dots, whiskers, hair strands, thin lips, tiny teeth, narrow legs, fragile joints, small holes, isolated islands, or floating fragments.
>
> Composition: exactly one centered compact near-square animal emblem, balanced at token scale. Pure solid black artwork on a pure white background with hard crisp edges. No grayscale, shading, gradients, texture, shadows, colour, token border, enclosing circle, frame, lettering, caption, numbers, or text.

The following subject block was appended to the shared prompt for each image:

- **Bear:** Show one front-facing natural bear head and short neck. Use two thick rounded ears with two large inner-ear openings, a very broad forehead, exactly two large solid eyes with no pupils, a massive short muzzle, and one single solid nose with no nostril holes. Closed mouth represented by one broad gap. Use only two or three large cheek-fur points per side. No visible teeth, claws, body, armour, collar, scars, or supernatural expression.
- **Boar:** Show one front-facing wild boar head and short neck. Use two thick outward-pointing ears, exactly two large solid eyes with no pupils, a broad wedge-shaped forehead, one massive rounded snout with exactly two very large nostril openings, and exactly two oversized upward-curving tusks firmly fused to the cheeks. Add only a few large bristle points along the top. No small teeth, body, legs, armour, collar, or extra tusks.
- **Giant Rat:** Show one unmistakable rat in a compact side-profile emblem: rounded body, pointed muzzle, one large rounded ear with one broad opening, one single solid eye with no pupil, four short thick legs fused close to the body, and one very thick curling tail connected continuously to the rump and body silhouette. No whiskers, tiny toes, separate paws, teeth, fur lines, cheese, or second animal.
- **Giant Spider:** Show one top-down giant spider icon with one large abdomen, one smaller head, and exactly eight very thick short legs. Each leg must use only one broad bend and remain strongly fused into the body; opposing legs may visually merge into sturdy pairs. Add exactly two large eye openings total, not many eyes. No web, hairs, fangs, thin leg tips, tiny joints, markings, or detached parts.
- **Giant Snake:** Show one top-down coiled giant snake forming one thick continuous spiral with a large raised head at the upper centre. Use one broad coil only, exactly two large solid eyes with no pupils, and one wide mouth gap. The head and coil must be fused into a single sturdy silhouette. No forked tongue, scales, fangs, tiny inner coils, thin tail tip, spots, or floating pieces.
- **Giant Bat:** Show one front-facing giant bat with a large simple head centred between two very broad spread wings. Use two thick pointed ears with large openings and exactly two large solid eyes with no pupils. Each wing should use only three broad membrane sections and a thick outer edge; wings and head must form one connected silhouette. No tiny claws, thin wing fingers, feet, fangs, fur lines, extra membrane ribs, or detached parts.
- **Giant Scorpion:** Show one simplified top-down giant scorpion with a broad segmented body, exactly two massive rounded pincers, four visible short blocky side legs total, and one very thick tail curling over the body with a large blunt stinger. All parts must be fused into one sturdy silhouette. Use at most three broad body divisions. No many thin legs, tiny joints, small claws, narrow tail tip, surface markings, or detached parts.
- **Giant Toad:** Show one front-facing crouching giant toad as a compact full-body emblem. Use a very broad head, exactly two huge solid eyes with no pupils, one wide mouth gap, a squat central body, and four thick folded limbs fused tightly into the body silhouette. Use only a few broad bumps on the outer silhouette. No tiny toes, tongue, spots, wart dots, thin legs, teeth, crown, or detached parts.

The technical conversion does not use dilation, erosion, hole filling, island removal, or artwork thickening. Only the Giant Snake used alternate trace sampling parameters to obtain clean manifold triangulation.
