# Clerics — Image generation prompts

Generated with the built-in ImageGen tool.

## Shared direction

Use case: logo-brand. Asset type: family-friendly classic-fantasy adventurer or NPC marker artwork for a flush two-colour 25 mm FDM token. Create one original humanoid class emblem matching a bold compact token collection as exactly one front-facing head-and-shoulders figure in one connected near-square solid-black silhouette, with two large eye openings and only large negative spaces. Designed around 18 mm for a 0.4 mm FDM nozzle and direct tracing without dilation, erosion, cleanup, hole filling, island removal, or line thickening. Every weapon, symbol, bridge, and opening must be about 6% of artwork width or wider and fused to the main silhouette. Pure black on opaque white with hard edges. No grayscale, transparency, shading, colour, token circle, border, text, scenery, injury imagery, tiny jewellery, thin hair strands, narrow blades, isolated islands, floating pieces, spell particles, or second character.

## Creature requests

- **Healer Cleric:** Calm hooded cleric with a broad high collar, one thick staff fused to a shoulder and one large plus-shaped opening in its top. No spell effects.
- **Battle Cleric:** Armored cleric with a broad nasal helmet, one short thick mace fused behind one shoulder and a large shield fused behind the other with one broad diamond opening.
- **Sun Cleric:** Robed cleric with a broad six-rayed sun crown fused into the hood and one thick sun-topped staff fused to a shoulder with one large circular opening. No rays detached.
- **Grave Cleric:** Solemn hooded cleric with a high angular collar, one short thick lantern staff fused to a shoulder with exactly one large opening and a broad crescent chest symbol cutout. No skulls.
