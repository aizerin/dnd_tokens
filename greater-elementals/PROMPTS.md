# Greater Elementals — Image generation prompts

Generated with the built-in ImageGen tool.

## Shared direction

Use case: logo-brand. Asset type: family-friendly classic-fantasy tabletop game marker artwork for a flush two-colour 25 mm FDM token. Create one original fantasy creature emblem matching a bold compact token collection as exactly one connected near-square solid-black silhouette with only large negative spaces. Designed around 18 mm for a 0.4 mm FDM nozzle and direct tracing without dilation, erosion, cleanup, hole filling, island removal, or line thickening. Every tentacle, crest, flow, bridge, and opening must be about 6% of artwork width or wider and connected. Pure black on opaque white with hard edges. No grayscale, transparency, shading, colour, token circle, border, text, scenery, injury imagery, gore, tiny texture, thin tendrils, narrow lines, isolated islands, floating pieces, or second creature.

## Creature requests

- **Earth Elemental:** One front-facing massive stone humanoid head and torso formed from exactly six broad rock plates fused together, two large eye openings, broad mouth gap and two huge shoulders. No loose rocks.
- **Fire Elemental:** One front-facing humanoid flame spirit head and torso formed by exactly seven broad rounded flame lobes fused into one body, two large eye openings and three large body gaps. No separate sparks.
- **Water Elemental:** One front-facing humanoid water spirit head and torso formed by exactly four huge curling wave bands that loop and reconnect, two large eye openings and three large body gaps. No droplets.
