# Elves Artwork Prompts

All four final images were created with the built-in image generator. An initial ornamental draft was rejected before conversion. The final prompts deliberately limit each glyph to one dominant black mass and no more than seven broad white cutouts.

## Elf Warrior

> Use case: logo-brand.
>
> Asset type: source artwork for a flush two-colour 25 mm FDM-printed tabletop token.
>
> Primary request: one completely new original ELF WARRIOR pictogram.
>
> Style/medium: extremely minimal bold stencil logo. The face, hair, ears, armour and sword form one dominant solid black mass. This is a filled black pictogram, NOT line art, NOT outline art, NOT a colouring-book illustration. Use graphic simplicity comparable to a restroom symbol or road-sign animal icon.
>
> Subject: exactly one front-facing fantasy elf head and shoulders. Very long sturdy pointed ears, narrow elegant jaw, one smooth swept-back hair mass, two broad shoulder armour lobes, and one oversized thick leaf-shaped sword rising diagonally behind one shoulder and fully merged into the bust. Elven but practical, noble and stern.
>
> Negative-space budget: exactly two large almond-shaped white eye openings; exactly two large inner-ear openings; one broad simple white mouth gap; at most two broad armour gaps. The entire image must contain no more than seven white cutouts. The face itself must remain a solid black mass. No white face fill and no black outline around a white face.
>
> Manufacturing constraints: final glyph is approximately 18 mm wide, printed with a 0.4 mm FDM nozzle and traced directly without later dilation, erosion, cleanup or thickening. Every black bridge and every white opening must be extremely broad, at least 5% of the complete glyph width. No thin slits, fragile tips or narrow internal lines. Sword firmly connected to the shoulder.
>
> Composition: one centered compact near-square emblem with generous white margin. Pure solid black artwork on pure white background with crisp hard edges.
>
> Avoid: pupils, eye highlights, eyebrows, nose or nostril marks, chin mark, hair highlights, hair strands, face outline, armour outlines, decorative lines, gems, runes, filigree, teeth, beard, hood, crown, bow, arrow, spear, shield, staff, floating fragments, extra characters, token rim, circle, border, text, letters, numbers, grayscale, colour, shading, texture, shadow or 3D rendering.

## Shared prompt for the remaining roles

> Use case: logo-brand.
>
> Asset type: source artwork for a flush two-colour 25 mm FDM-printed tabletop token.
>
> Style/medium: extremely minimal bold stencil logo matching a simple established Elf Warrior token. The face, hair, ears, clothing and requested equipment form one dominant solid black mass. This is a filled black pictogram, NOT line art, NOT outline art, NOT a colouring-book illustration. Use graphic simplicity comparable to a restroom symbol or road-sign animal icon.
>
> Shared elf identity: exactly one front-facing fantasy elf head and shoulders; very long sturdy pointed ears; narrow elegant jaw; one smooth swept-back hair mass; two broad shoulder shapes. The elf is capable, noble and stern.
>
> Negative-space budget: exactly two large almond-shaped white eye openings; exactly two large inner-ear openings; one broad simple white mouth gap; at most two additional broad clothing or equipment openings. Maximum seven white cutouts in the entire image. The face must remain a solid black mass. No white face fill and no black outline around a white face.
>
> Manufacturing constraints: final glyph is approximately 18 mm wide, printed with a 0.4 mm FDM nozzle and traced directly without later dilation, erosion, cleanup, hole filling, island removal or thickening. Every black bridge and every white opening must be extremely broad, at least 5% of the complete glyph width. No thin slits, fragile tips or narrow internal lines. Equipment must be fully merged into the shoulder silhouette.
>
> Composition: one centered compact near-square emblem with generous white margin. Pure solid black artwork on pure white background with crisp hard edges.
>
> Avoid: pupils, eye highlights, eyebrows, nose or nostril marks, chin mark, hair highlights, hair strands, face outline, clothing outlines, armour outlines, decorative lines, gems, runes, filigree, teeth, beard, floating fragments, extra characters, token rim, circle, border, text, letters, numbers, grayscale, colour, shading, texture, shadow or 3D rendering.

The following role block was appended to the shared prompt for each image:

- **Elf Archer:** Add a simple close hood mass resting behind the long ears and one oversized very thick arrow crossing diagonally behind the head, fully merged into the shoulders. The broad arrowhead and broad fletching must be immediately readable as solid silhouettes. Add one large leaf-shaped cloak clasp as a single solid protrusion, not an outlined decoration. No bow, sword, spear, shield, crown, staff or quiver.
- **Elf Spearman:** Add simple broad shoulder armour and one oversized very thick spear held vertically beside one shoulder, fully merged into the bust. The spear has one broad solid leaf-shaped head and a thick shaft. Add one simple solid low forehead guard with no interior decoration. No sword, bow, arrow, shield, crown, hood or staff.
- **Elf Mage:** Add a massive high robe collar and one very thick staff rising beside one shoulder, fully merged into the bust. The staff top is one large solid crescent with a single very broad central opening and a thick connection to the shaft. Add one simple solid diamond-shaped forehead protrusion integrated into the hairline, not a floating gem. No spell effects, stars, flame, sword, bow, arrow, spear, shield or crown.

The technical conversion does not use dilation, erosion, hole filling, island removal or artwork thickening. Transparent source backgrounds are composited onto pure white before tracing without changing the glyph shapes. Only the Elf Warrior used alternate trace sampling parameters to obtain clean manifold triangulation.
