# Oozes — Image Generation Prompts

Built-in ImageGen was used separately for every token.

## Shared prompt

> Use case: logo-brand. One abstract living slime as a compact connected solid-black emblem for a flush two-colour 25 mm FDM token, with only a few large rounded bubble openings and no humanoid face. Designed around 18 mm for a 0.4 mm nozzle and direct tracing without dilation, erosion, cleanup, hole filling, island removal or thickening. Pure black on opaque white; no shading, colour, border, text, thin drips, tiny bubbles, narrow tendrils or floating pieces.

## Role blocks

- **Gelatinous Cube:** Rounded square upright mass with diagonal bubbles and one central void.
- **Gray Ooze:** Low wide puddle with five lobes, a fused curled pseudopod and two bubbles.
- **Ochre Jelly:** Tall mound with one broad groove, three fused pseudopods and two bubbles.
- **Black Pudding:** Broad scalloped mound with a fused raised arc and four bubbles.
