# Class NPC Production Plan

This production run adds 48 adventurer and NPC tokens in 12 class folders.

- Fighters: Sword & Shield Fighter, Greatsword Fighter, Spear Fighter, Crossbow Fighter
- Clerics: Healer Cleric, Battle Cleric, Sun Cleric, Grave Cleric
- Adventurer Wizards: Generalist Wizard, Elementalist Wizard, Illusionist Wizard, Diviner Wizard
- Rogues: Thief, Assassin, Scout, Swashbuckler
- Rangers: Archer Ranger, Beastmaster Ranger, Monster Hunter Ranger, Warden Ranger
- Paladins: Guardian Paladin, Crusader Paladin, Oathkeeper Paladin, Avenger Paladin
- Barbarians: Berserker, Raider, Totem Warrior, Chieftain
- Bards: Minstrel, Skald, Court Bard, Battle Bard
- Druids: Herbalist Druid, Shapeshifter Druid, Forest Druid, Archdruid
- Monks: Initiate Monk, Martial Artist, Staff Monk, Monk Master
- Sorcerers: Draconic Sorcerer, Wild Magic Sorcerer, Storm Sorcerer, Shadow Sorcerer
- Warlocks: Fiend Pact Warlock, Fey Pact Warlock, Eldritch Pact Warlock, Hexblade Warlock

All categories use English names, direct source tracing without morphology, 25 mm diameter, 1.0 mm total thickness, 0.4 mm colour inlay on both faces, a 0.2 mm centre core, base on extruder 2, and artwork plus perimeter ring on extruder 1.
