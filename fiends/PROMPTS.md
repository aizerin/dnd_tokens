# Fiends — Image generation prompts

Generated with the built-in ImageGen tool. All source artwork uses the following shared direction, followed by the creature-specific request.

## Shared direction

Use case: logo-brand. Family-friendly classic-fantasy tabletop marker artwork for a flush two-colour 25 mm FDM token. Create one original creature emblem as one connected, near-square solid-black silhouette with only large negative spaces. Design around 18 mm for a 0.4 mm FDM nozzle and direct tracing without dilation, erosion, cleanup, hole filling, island removal, or line thickening. Every feature and bridge must be about 5% of artwork width or wider. Pure black on opaque white with hard edges. No grayscale, transparency, shading, colour, token circle, border, text, scenery, injury imagery, or second creature.

## Imp

One front-facing small winged fiend head-and-shoulders emblem with a narrow face, two short thick horns, pointed ears, two large eye openings, and exactly two broad bat wings fused to the head and shoulders. Each wing has one large opening. One thick tail curls up and reconnects to a wing. No trident, many teeth, or flame.

## Quasit

One front-facing small squat fiend head-and-shoulders emblem with a broad wrinkled face represented by only three large gaps, two thick backward horns, pointed ears, two large eyes, and a high hunched collar. One thick tail curls around and reconnects to the collar. No wings, weapon, many teeth, or flame.

## Lemure

One squat, harmless-looking amorphous fantasy minion as a compact full-body silhouette. Huge rounded head merged into a broad blob-like body, two very short thick arms fused to its sides, two short fused feet, two large eye openings, a broad nose opening, one thick closed mouth gap, and exactly three large rounded body-fold openings. No horns, wings, tail, claws, spikes, teeth, weapons, or flames.

## Hell Hound

One front-facing supernatural hound head-and-shoulders emblem with tall pointed ears, huge angular muzzle, two large eye openings, broad nose, thick closed mouth gap, and exactly four broad mane points. A thick tail-like arc reconnects behind one shoulder. No open mouth, teeth, fire, smoke, collar, or second head.
