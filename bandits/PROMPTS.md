# Bandit Artwork Prompts

The original Bandit artwork predates this category expansion. The three new images were created with the built-in image generator, using the original Bandit only as a faction and collection style reference.

## Shared faction prompt

> Use case: logo-brand. Asset type: source artwork for a flush two-colour 25 mm FDM-printed tabletop token. Input image: the supplied Bandit is the primary faction and collection style reference. Create a completely new original role glyph belonging to the same human outlaw gang. Preserve the faction language: compact front-facing head-and-shoulders emblem, threatening eyes, face covering, ragged clothing, and bold simplified black-and-white shapes.
>
> Manufacturing constraint: the artwork will be reduced to approximately 18 mm wide and converted directly to SVG for two-colour FDM printing with a 0.4 mm nozzle, without later dilation, erosion, cleanup, or line thickening. Use very thick connected shapes, very few large negative spaces, and generous clear gaps. Every essential black bridge, white gap, outline, facial opening, and role attribute must be about 5% of the artwork width or thicker. Avoid narrow necks, fragile spikes, tiny fingers, thin weapon strings, small holes, thin slits, isolated islands, and floating pieces.
>
> Composition: exactly one centered compact near-square pictogram, visually balanced at token scale. Pure solid black artwork on a pure white background with crisp hard edges. No grayscale, shading, gradients, texture, shadows, colour, token border, enclosing circle, frame, lettering, caption, numbers, or text.

The following role block was appended to the shared prompt for each image:

- **Bandit Archer:** Primary request: BANDIT ARCHER. Keep the hood and face covering, but replace the sword with one oversized thick arrow crossing diagonally behind the head and fused into the shoulder silhouette. The broad arrowhead and broad fletching must be unmistakable at small size. No bow, no quiver, no sword, no additional arrows, and no fine straps.
- **Bandit Brawler:** Primary request: BANDIT BRAWLER. Show a stocky human outlaw with a lower hood or heavy head wrap, the same face covering and ragged shoulder cloak, plus two oversized wrapped clenched fists raised beside the face and fused firmly into the shoulders. The fists should be simple blocky silhouettes with only one or two broad separations, not anatomically detailed. No weapon, shield, arrow, hat, rings, or tiny fingers.
- **Bandit Captain:** Primary request: BANDIT CAPTAIN. Show an experienced commanding human outlaw wearing a broad wide-brimmed brigand hat above the face covering, a heavy high collar, and one large round rank clasp at the throat. Add one bold scar notch through one eyebrow. The hat and collar must dominate the silhouette and remain fused to the bust. No crown, no weapon, no arrow, no feather, no tiny jewellery, and no decorative micro-detail.

The technical conversion only traces the artwork boundary. It does not use dilation, erosion, hole filling, island removal, or artwork thickening.
