## Shared direction

Use case: logo-brand. Asset type: family-friendly classic-fantasy tabletop marker artwork for a flush two-colour 25 mm FDM token. Create one original creature emblem matching a bold compact token collection as exactly one connected near-square solid-black silhouette with only large negative spaces. Designed around 18 mm for a 0.4 mm FDM nozzle and direct tracing without dilation, erosion, cleanup, hole filling, island removal, or line thickening. Every limb, tentacle, bridge, and opening must be about 6% of artwork width or wider and connected. Pure black on opaque white with hard edges. No grayscale, transparency, shading, colour, token circle, border, text, scenery, injury imagery, tiny texture, thin tendrils, narrow lines, isolated islands, floating pieces, or second creature.
