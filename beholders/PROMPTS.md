# Beholders — Image generation prompts

Generated with the built-in ImageGen tool.

## Shared direction

Use case: logo-brand. Asset type: family-friendly classic-fantasy tabletop game marker artwork for a flush two-colour 25 mm FDM token. Create one original non-graphic fantasy creature emblem matching a bold compact token collection as exactly one connected near-square solid-black silhouette with only large negative spaces. Designed around 18 mm for a 0.4 mm FDM nozzle and direct tracing without dilation, erosion, cleanup, hole filling, island removal, or line thickening. Every crown point, stalk, bridge, and opening must be about 6% of artwork width or wider and connected. Pure black on opaque white with hard edges. No grayscale, transparency, shading, colour, token circle, border, text, scenery, injury imagery, gore, tiny texture, thin spikes, narrow lines, isolated islands, floating pieces, or second creature.

## Creature requests

- **Beholder:** One huge spherical eye creature: one very large central eye opening, broad closed mouth gap and exactly eight short thick eye stalks that curve back and reconnect to the round body. No teeth.
- **Spectator:** One smaller spherical eye creature: one huge central eye opening, broad closed mouth gap and exactly four short thick eye stalks reconnected to the body, plus two broad side fins.
- **Gauth:** One angular floating eye creature: one huge diamond-shaped central eye, broad closed mouth gap and exactly six short thick eye stalks reconnected to a faceted body. No teeth.
- **Death Tyrant:** One stylized undead spherical eye tyrant in non-graphic mask form: one huge central eye opening, broad closed jaw gap, exactly eight thick crown-like eye stalks reconnected to the body and four large shell openings.
