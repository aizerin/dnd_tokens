# Lizardfolk — Image Generation Prompts

Built-in ImageGen was used separately for every token.

## Shared prompt

> Use case: logo-brand. Source artwork for a flush two-colour 25 mm FDM token. One front-facing reptilian humanoid with a long broad snout, heavy brow, large eyes, thick cheek plates, small neck frill and broad shoulders. One compact solid-black silhouette with one thick item fused behind a shoulder. No repeated scales. Designed around 18 mm for a 0.4 mm nozzle and traced without dilation, erosion, cleanup, hole filling, island removal or thickening. Pure black on opaque white; no shading, colour, token border, text, thin teeth, tongue, scale texture, tiny details or floating pieces.

## Role blocks

- **Lizardfolk Warrior:** Low hornless plate helmet, shoulder collar and massive stone club.
- **Lizardfolk Hunter:** Three-point swept crest, hide collar and broad hunting spear.
- **Lizardfolk Brute:** Bare square snout, massive shoulder plates and stone axe.
- **Lizardfolk Shaman:** Ceremonial hood, high robe collar and sun-disc staff.
