# Magic & Objectives — Image generation prompts

Generated with the built-in ImageGen tool.

## Shared direction

Use case: logo-brand. Asset type: family-friendly classic-fantasy dungeon object or objective marker artwork for a flush two-colour 25 mm FDM token. Create one original object emblem matching a bold compact token collection as exactly one connected near-square solid-black silhouette with only large negative spaces. Use a clear front or top-down view. Designed around 18 mm for a 0.4 mm FDM nozzle and direct tracing without dilation, erosion, cleanup, hole filling, island removal, or line thickening. Every support, rune band, board, bridge, and opening must be about 7% of artwork width or wider and fused to the main silhouette. Pure black on opaque white with hard edges. No grayscale, transparency, shading, colour, token circle outside the object, border, text, scenery, tiny runes, thin chains, individual coins, narrow lines, isolated islands, floating pieces, people, or creatures.

## Creature requests

- **Magic Portal:** One upright oval stone portal in front view: thick outer arch fused to a broad base, exactly four large stone openings and one huge central swirling opening made from two thick connected curves. No particles.
- **Ritual Circle:** One top-down ritual circle emblem: two thick concentric bands connected by exactly four broad bridges, one large central diamond opening and exactly four large outer rune-shaped openings. No tiny symbols.
- **Crystal Orb:** One huge faceted crystal orb fused into a broad three-legged stand: exactly four large facet openings, thick circular rim and no glow or particles.
- **Treasure Pile:** One compact connected mound of treasure: one broad chest shape fused into exactly four huge coin-mound lobes and two large gem openings. No individual coins or loose objects.
