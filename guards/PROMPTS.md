# Guards — Image Generation Prompts

The source artwork was created with the built-in image generator. Each token was generated separately with the shared prompt and one role block.

## Shared prompt

> Use case: logo-brand.
>
> Asset type: source artwork for a flush two-colour 25 mm FDM-printed tabletop token.
>
> Collection style: create one completely new original classic-fantasy HUMAN GUARD pictogram matching a bold compact head-and-shoulders tabletop-token collection. Use a strong graphic silhouette, readable human face, oversized role-defining helmet or headgear, and exactly one short piece of equipment integrated behind one shoulder. This is not realistic portrait art, not line art, not a heraldic crest and not an isolated object.
>
> Composition: exactly one front-facing human guard shown as a compact near-square head-and-shoulders emblem. Use one dominant solid-black silhouette. Keep the equipment short, very thick, close to the shoulder, and firmly fused into the bust. Leave a generous white margin around the complete glyph.
>
> Face openings: exactly two large simple eye openings with no pupils, one broad simple nose opening, and one short thick neutral horizontal mouth gap. The mouth must be a plain closed slit, not lips, not a smile, not a frown, not an open mouth, and no teeth. Add at most three other broad helmet, hood or armour gaps. Maximum seven white openings total.
>
> Manufacturing constraints: final glyph is approximately 18 mm wide on a 25 mm token and printed with a 0.4 mm FDM nozzle. It will be traced directly without dilation, erosion, cleanup, hole filling, island removal or line thickening. Every black bridge, equipment connector and white opening must be about 5% of the artwork width or wider. No thin visor bars, chainmail texture, narrow weapon shafts, fragile points, tiny rivets, facial wrinkles, hair strands, cloth folds, insignia, runes, isolated islands or floating pieces.
>
> Output: pure solid black artwork on a pure opaque white background with hard crisp edges. No grayscale, transparency, shading, gradients, texture, shadow, colour, token circle, rim, border, frame, text, letters or numbers. No shield, horse, animal, second character or unrequested equipment.

## City Guard role block

> Primary request: CITY GUARD. Show a dependable ordinary town watchman wearing one broad open-faced kettle helmet with a thick downturned brim and one heavy padded collar. Add one short very thick spear behind one shoulder, fused into the helmet brim or collar, ending in one oversized blunt leaf-shaped spearhead. Keep the face clean-shaven and alert but neutral. No hood, plume, beard, moustache, bow, sword, halberd or crown.

## Guard Archer role block

> Primary request: GUARD ARCHER. Show a watchful town archer wearing one simple close-fitting hood beneath a low rounded helmet. Add one short oversized arrow diagonally behind one shoulder, with a very thick shaft, one broad triangular arrowhead and one simplified solid fletching block, all firmly fused into the bust. Keep the face clean-shaven and focused. No kettle helmet, plume, beard, moustache, bow, sword, spear or crown.

## Veteran Guard role block

> Primary request: VETERAN GUARD. Show a seasoned older infantry guard wearing one heavy angular nasal helmet with broad cheek guards and one high segmented armour collar. Give him one short broad moustache made from exactly two thick lobes. Add one short very thick sword behind one shoulder, fused into the collar, with one broad blunt blade and a simple oversized crossguard. Use a stern neutral expression. No plume, hood, beard, spear, bow, axe, shield or crown.

## Guard Captain role block

> Primary request: GUARD CAPTAIN. Show a commanding professional guard captain wearing one tall crested helmet with exactly one large backward-sweeping solid plume and a broad formal gorget. Give him one short squared beard fused into the chin and collar. Add one short very thick command baton behind one shoulder, fused into the gorget, with one broad blunt round top. Use a confident neutral expression. No crown, hood, moustache, long beard, spear, sword, bow, shield or decorative insignia.
