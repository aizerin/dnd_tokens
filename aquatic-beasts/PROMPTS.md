# Aquatic Beasts — Image generation prompts

Generated with the built-in ImageGen tool.

## Shared direction

Use case: logo-brand. Asset type: family-friendly classic-fantasy tabletop game marker artwork for a flush two-colour 25 mm FDM token. Create one original creature emblem matching a bold compact token collection as exactly one connected near-square solid-black silhouette with only large negative spaces. Designed around 18 mm for a 0.4 mm FDM nozzle and direct tracing without dilation, erosion, cleanup, hole filling, island removal, or line thickening. Every limb, wing, tail, bridge, and opening must be about 6% of artwork width or wider and connected. Pure black on opaque white with hard edges. No grayscale, transparency, shading, colour, token circle, border, text, scenery, injury imagery, tiny feathers or scales, thin spikes, narrow lines, isolated islands, floating pieces, or second creature.

- **Giant Octopus:** One front-facing octopus with huge rounded head, two large eye openings and exactly eight short thick tentacles arranged as a connected skirt with six large gaps.
- **Reef Shark:** One compact side-profile shark with broad triangular body, one large eye, exactly three thick fins, a thick tail fused back by a water-like base arc and two broad gill openings.
- **Crocodile:** One top-view compact crocodile with a broad long snout, two large eye openings, exactly four short legs fused to the body and a thick curved tail reconnected to one hind leg.
