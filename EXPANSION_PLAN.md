# Expanded Bestiary Production Plan

This production run adds 110 tokens in 27 category folders.

## Humanoid societies

Drow, Duergar, Troglodytes, Bullywugs, Kuo-toa, Sahuagin, Myconids, Yuan-ti.

## Missing creature types

Aberrations, Minor Elementals, Plant Creatures, Swarms & Vermin.

## Existing-family expansions

Monstrosities II, Monstrosities III, Fey II, Fiends II, Undead II, Lycanthropes, Beasts II, Aquatic Beasts, Dragonkin.

## Iconic and high-level sets

Liches, Beholders, Mind Flayers, Greater Elementals, Chromatic Dragons, Metallic Dragons.

All categories use English names, direct source tracing without morphology, 25 mm diameter, 1.0 mm total thickness, 0.4 mm colour inlay on both faces, a 0.2 mm centre core, base on extruder 2, and artwork plus perimeter ring on extruder 1.
