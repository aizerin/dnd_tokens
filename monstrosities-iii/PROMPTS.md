# Monstrosities III — Image generation prompts

Generated with the built-in ImageGen tool.

## Shared direction

Use case: logo-brand. Asset type: family-friendly classic-fantasy tabletop game marker artwork for a flush two-colour 25 mm FDM token. Create one original creature emblem matching a bold compact token collection as exactly one connected near-square solid-black silhouette with only large negative spaces. Designed around 18 mm for a 0.4 mm FDM nozzle and direct tracing without dilation, erosion, cleanup, hole filling, island removal, or line thickening. Every limb, wing, horn, bridge, and opening must be about 6% of artwork width or wider and connected. Pure black on opaque white with hard edges. No grayscale, transparency, shading, colour, token circle, border, text, scenery, injury imagery, tiny texture, thin spikes, narrow lines, isolated islands, floating pieces, or second creature.

- **Ankheg:** One front-facing armored burrowing insect head-and-shoulders emblem: huge paired mandibles, two large eyes, exactly four broad shell plates and two short thick antennae fused back to the head.
- **Ettercap:** One front-facing spider humanoid head and shoulders: broad mask-like face, exactly four large eye openings, two thick jaw hooks and exactly four broad shoulder-leg arcs reconnected to the body.
- **Displacer Beast:** One side-profile panther-like creature with exactly four short fused legs, one large eye, a thick curled tail reconnected to the back and exactly two huge shoulder tentacles that loop and reconnect. No thin tips.
- **Manticore:** One front-facing lion-like head and shoulders with two broad bat wings fused behind, two large eyes, exactly four broad mane points and a thick scorpion tail arch reconnected behind one wing.
