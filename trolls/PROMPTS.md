# Trolls — Image Generation Prompts

Built-in ImageGen was used separately for every token.

## Shared prompt

> Use case: logo-brand. Source artwork for a flush two-colour 25 mm FDM token. Create one original classic-fantasy troll: long angular face, hooked nose, pointed ears, heavy brow, shaggy fused hair masses, thick neck and hunched shoulders. One front-facing near-square solid-black silhouette with one short thick item fused behind a shoulder. Two large eye openings, broad nose, closed mouth and two blunt lower fangs. Designed at approximately 18 mm for a 0.4 mm nozzle and direct tracing without dilation, erosion, cleanup, hole filling, island removal or line thickening. Pure black on opaque white; no grayscale, shading, colour, token border, text, tiny details, floating pieces, blood or gore.

## Role blocks

- **Cave Troll:** Bald crown, jutting brow, ragged stone-hide collar and massive rough stone club.
- **Forest Troll:** Thick swept-back leaf-shaped hair crest, bark-like collar and massive branch spear.
- **Swamp Troll:** Broad drooping marsh hood, rounded collar and massive hooked pole.
- **Troll Witch:** Crooked pointed hood, heavy chin beard, high collar and ritual staff with one broad spiral opening.
