# Myconids — Image generation prompts

Generated with the built-in ImageGen tool.

## Shared direction

Use case: logo-brand. Asset type: family-friendly classic-fantasy tabletop game marker artwork for a flush two-colour 25 mm FDM token. Designed around 18 mm for a 0.4 mm FDM nozzle and direct tracing without dilation, erosion, cleanup, hole filling, island removal, or line thickening. Exactly one front-facing head-and-shoulders figure as one connected near-square solid-black silhouette with two large eye openings and only large negative spaces. Every feature, weapon, bridge, and opening must be about 5% of artwork width or wider and connected. Pure black on opaque white with hard edges. No grayscale, transparency, shading, colour, token circle, border, text, scenery, injury imagery, tiny texture, thin spikes, narrow lines, isolated islands, floating pieces, or second creature. Create one original Myconids emblem matching a bold compact token collection. Subject anatomy: mushroom humanoid with a broad cap, simple face and stout shoulders.

## Creature requests

- **Myconid Sprout:** Small rounded cap with exactly three broad spots, very short body and two stubby arms fused to the torso.
- **Myconid Adult:** Wide flat cap with exactly four broad spots, long simple face and exactly three thick gill openings.
- **Myconid Guard:** Heavy angular cap, thick neck and one short broad wooden club fused behind one shoulder.
- **Myconid Sovereign:** Huge crown-shaped cap with exactly five broad lobes, high collar of thick gills and no weapon.
