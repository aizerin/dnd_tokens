# Bugbears — Image Generation Prompts

Built-in ImageGen was used separately for every token.

## Shared prompt

> Use case: logo-brand. Source artwork for a flush two-colour 25 mm FDM token. Create one original large shaggy bugbear with a broad flat face, wide jaw, small round ears, blunt muzzle and thick cheek and shoulder fur. One front-facing compact solid-black silhouette with one thick item fused behind a shoulder. Two large eyes, broad nose, closed mouth and two blunt lower fangs. Designed around 18 mm for a 0.4 mm nozzle and traced without dilation, erosion, cleanup, hole filling, island removal or thickening. Pure black on opaque white; no shading, colour, border, text, tiny details or floating pieces.

## Role blocks

- **Bugbear Warrior:** Leather helmet, fur collar and solid four-bump morningstar without a chain.
- **Bugbear Ambusher:** Close hood, asymmetrical fur and oversized javelin.
- **Bugbear Chief:** Tall hornless war helmet with a backward crest and massive axe.
- **Bugbear Shaman:** Crooked hood, chin beard, high collar and crescent staff.
