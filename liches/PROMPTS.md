# Liches — Image generation prompts

Generated with the built-in ImageGen tool.

## Shared direction

Use case: logo-brand. Asset type: family-friendly classic-fantasy tabletop game marker artwork for a flush two-colour 25 mm FDM token. Create one original non-graphic fantasy creature emblem matching a bold compact token collection as exactly one connected near-square solid-black silhouette with only large negative spaces. Designed around 18 mm for a 0.4 mm FDM nozzle and direct tracing without dilation, erosion, cleanup, hole filling, island removal, or line thickening. Every crown point, stalk, bridge, and opening must be about 6% of artwork width or wider and connected. Pure black on opaque white with hard edges. No grayscale, transparency, shading, colour, token circle, border, text, scenery, injury imagery, gore, tiny texture, thin spikes, narrow lines, isolated islands, floating pieces, or second creature.

## Creature requests

- **Lich:** One front-facing ancient skeletal sorcerer head and shoulders with two large eye openings, broad closed jaw, exactly four crown points, high robe collar and one thick staff fused to a shoulder. No exposed injury.
- **Archlich:** One front-facing regal undead mage head and shoulders with two large eye openings, broad mask-like face, exactly five crown points, huge ceremonial collar and a thick orb-topped staff fused to one shoulder with one large opening.
- **Demilich:** One floating stylized skull emblem fused into a broad crown-shaped aura base: two huge eye openings, broad nose opening, closed jaw gap and exactly six large blunt crown points. No separate gems or flames.
- **Dracolich:** One front-facing undead dragon skull emblem in symbolic non-graphic style: two huge eye openings, broad nasal opening, closed jaw gap, two thick backward horns and exactly four broad cheek plates fused to shoulders.
