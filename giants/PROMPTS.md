# Giants — Image Generation Prompts

Built-in ImageGen was used separately for every token. The Stone Giant used a family-friendly retry after one generated result was rejected by the image safety filter.

## Shared prompt

> Use case: logo-brand. Family-friendly tabletop marker artwork for a flush two-colour 25 mm FDM token. One enormous front-facing humanoid with broad head, large jaw, thick neck and huge shoulders, as a compact solid-black silhouette with one thick connected role prop. Designed around 18 mm for a 0.4 mm nozzle and direct tracing without dilation, erosion, cleanup, hole filling, island removal or thickening. Pure black on opaque white; no shading, colour, border, text, tiny details or floating pieces.

## Role blocks

- **Hill Giant:** Bald lumpy crown, huge rounded nose, hide collar and wooden mallet.
- **Stone Giant:** Tall angular stone head, geometric mantle and connected stone orb.
- **Frost Giant:** Northern helmet, three-point beard, fur collar and double-headed axe.
- **Fire Giant:** Heavy forge helmet, clean square face, plate collar and forge hammer.
