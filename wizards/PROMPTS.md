# Wizards — Image Generation Prompts

The source artwork was created with the built-in image generator. Each token was generated as a separate image so every creature could receive a precise role-specific description while sharing the same FDM-safe collection style.

## Apprentice Wizard

> Use case: logo-brand.
>
> Asset type: style-test source artwork for a flush two-colour 25 mm FDM-printed tabletop token.
>
> Primary request: create one completely new original APPRENTICE WIZARD pictogram in the same visual language as a bold fantasy elf head-and-shoulders token: a compact character portrait with equipment integrated into the silhouette, strong readable negative spaces, and slightly elegant fantasy styling. This is not the simplified isolated-head style.
>
> Subject: exactly one front-facing young adult human wizard apprentice shown as a compact head-and-shoulders emblem. Give the character one oversized floppy pointed hat with a broad curved brim, a single thick folded tip, and no stars or decorations. Show a narrow alert face, short solid hair masses below the hat, a high simple cloak collar, and one short oversized wand crossing diagonally behind one shoulder and fused firmly into the bust. The wand ends in one broad blunt diamond shape, not a tiny star. Make the character clever, inexperienced and serious rather than comic.
>
> Face openings: exactly two large almond-shaped eye openings with no pupils, one broad simple nose opening, and one short thick neutral horizontal mouth gap. The mouth must be a plain closed stern slit, not lips, not a smile, not a frown, not an open mouth, and no teeth. Add at most two other large cloak or hat gaps. Maximum seven white openings total.
>
> Manufacturing constraints: final glyph is approximately 18 mm wide on a 25 mm token and printed with a 0.4 mm FDM nozzle. It will be traced directly without later dilation, erosion, cleanup, hole filling, island removal or line thickening. Use one dominant solid-black silhouette, broad equipment connectors and only large negative spaces. Every black bridge and every white opening must be about 5% of the artwork width or wider. No thin hat lines, narrow wand, fragile tip, tiny face details, hair strands, cloth folds, runes, sparks, stars, isolated islands or floating pieces.
>
> Composition: centered compact near-square head-and-shoulders portrait with generous white margin. Pure solid black artwork on a pure opaque white background with hard crisp edges. No grayscale, transparency, shading, gradients, texture, shadow, colour, token circle, rim, border, frame, text, letters or numbers. No beard, moustache, staff, book, potion, animal familiar, second character or extra equipment.

## Shared prompt for Hedge Wizard, Battle Mage, and Court Wizard

> Use case: logo-brand.
>
> Asset type: source artwork for a flush two-colour 25 mm FDM-printed tabletop token.
>
> Collection style: match an approved Apprentice Wizard token: a compact slightly elegant fantasy head-and-shoulders portrait with an oversized role-defining hat or hood, a short piece of equipment integrated behind one shoulder, and strong readable negative spaces. This is not an isolated-head monster symbol, not realistic portrait art, not line art and not a coat of arms.
>
> Composition: exactly one front-facing human wizard shown as a compact near-square head-and-shoulders emblem. Use one dominant solid-black silhouette. Keep equipment short, thick, close to the shoulders and firmly fused into the bust. Leave a generous white margin around the complete glyph.
>
> Face openings: exactly two large almond-shaped eye openings with no pupils, one broad simple nose opening, and one short thick neutral horizontal mouth gap. The mouth must be a plain closed slit, not lips, not a smile, not a frown, not an open mouth, and no teeth. Add at most three other broad hat, hood, beard or robe gaps. Maximum seven white openings total.
>
> Manufacturing constraints: final glyph is approximately 18 mm wide on a 25 mm token and printed with a 0.4 mm FDM nozzle. It will be traced directly without dilation, erosion, cleanup, hole filling, island removal or line thickening. Every black bridge, equipment connector and white opening must be about 5% of the artwork width or wider. No thin hat lines, narrow shafts, fragile points, tiny face details, individual hair strands, cloth folds, runes, sparks, stars, isolated islands or floating pieces.
>
> Output: pure solid black artwork on a pure opaque white background with hard crisp edges. No grayscale, transparency, shading, gradients, texture, shadow, colour, token circle, rim, border, frame, text, letters or numbers. No book, potion, animal familiar, second character or unrequested equipment.

### Hedge Wizard role block

> Primary request: HEDGE WIZARD. Show an old eccentric rural wizard with one oversized crooked pointed hat, a broad curved brim, and one thick folded tip. Give him a large bushy triangular beard fused firmly into the chest silhouette; the outer beard may have at most four oversized blunt points and no individual hair lines. Add one short very thick crooked wooden staff beside one shoulder, fused into the hat brim or robe, with one broad hooked top. Use a friendly but serious expression. No wand, hood, crown, armour, skull or spell effects.

### Battle Mage role block

> Primary request: BATTLE MAGE. Show a stern clean-shaven battle mage wearing one heavy angular hood and a massive high robe collar. The face must remain clearly human and shadowed by the hood. Add one short very thick staff beside one shoulder, fused into the collar, topped by one large solid diamond shape with no small hole. Use a severe neutral expression. No pointed hat, beard, moustache, crown, helmet, sword, shield, flame or floating spell effects.

### Court Wizard role block

> Primary request: COURT WIZARD. Show an older dignified royal adviser wearing one tall rounded scholar cap and a formal broad high collar. Give him one broad upturned moustache made from exactly two thick lobes and one short squared beard fused into the chin and collar. Add one short thick ceremonial wand behind one shoulder, fused into the cap or collar, ending in one broad blunt four-point shape with no small holes. Use a confident neutral expression. No pointed hat, hood, crown, armour, long beard or floating spell effects.
