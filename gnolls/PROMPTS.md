# Gnoll Artwork Prompts

All four images were created with the built-in image generator. The existing Werewolf, Orc Raider, and Hobgoblin Soldier artwork was used only as a broad collection style reference for the first token. The completed Gnoll Warrior became the primary faction reference for the remaining roles.

## Gnoll Warrior

> Create one new standalone bitmap source icon for a tabletop token, matching the bold minimal black-and-white pictogram language of the references but depicting a GNOLL WARRIOR.
>
> Subject: a front-facing hyena-headed gnoll warrior. Make the species unmistakably hyena-like: rounded hyena ears, sloping forehead, broad short muzzle, powerful heavy jaw, ragged mane, and a feral expression. It must be clearly distinct from a werewolf: no pointed wolf ears and no long canine snout. Add one broad crude cleaver or axe silhouette fused behind one shoulder to communicate the warrior role.
>
> Manufacturing constraint: this artwork will be reduced to approximately 18 mm wide and converted directly to SVG for two-colour FDM printing with a 0.4 mm nozzle, without later dilation, erosion, or line thickening. Therefore use only very thick connected shapes, very few large negative spaces, and generous clear gaps. Every essential black bridge, white gap, outline, facial opening, and weapon feature should be about 5% of the artwork width or thicker. Avoid narrow necks, fragile spikes, tiny teeth, whiskers, fur strands, small holes, thin slits, isolated islands, and floating pieces.
>
> Composition: centered, compact, near-square logo-brand pictogram, visually balanced at token scale. Pure solid black artwork on a pure white background only. Hard crisp edges. No grayscale, antialias-style shading, gradients, texture, shadows, colour, circular token border, enclosing circle, frame, lettering, caption, numbers, or text.

## Shared faction prompt for the remaining roles

> Match the supplied Gnoll Warrior's bold minimal black-and-white pictogram language and preserve the same faction identity: front-facing hyena head, large rounded hyena ears, sloping forehead, broad short muzzle, powerful heavy jaw, ragged mane, compact near-square bust. Clearly hyena-like, not a werewolf: no pointed wolf ears and no long canine snout.
>
> Manufacturing constraint: the artwork will be reduced to approximately 18 mm wide and converted directly to SVG for two-colour FDM printing with a 0.4 mm nozzle, without later dilation, erosion, cleanup, or line thickening. Use only very thick connected shapes, very few large negative spaces, and generous clear gaps. Every essential black bridge, white gap, outline, facial opening, and role attribute must be about 5% of the artwork width or thicker. Avoid narrow necks, fragile spikes, tiny teeth, whiskers, fur strands, small holes, thin slits, isolated islands, and floating pieces.
>
> Pure solid black artwork on a pure white background only, with hard crisp edges. No grayscale, shading, gradients, texture, shadows, colour, token border, enclosing circle, frame, lettering, caption, numbers, or text.

The following role block was appended to the shared prompt for each image:

- **Gnoll Hunter:** Create one new standalone bitmap source icon for a GNOLL HUNTER tabletop token. Give the gnoll a simple thick hunting headband and one oversized arrow silhouette diagonally behind the head, fused into the main silhouette so it cannot become a loose piece. The arrowhead and fletching must be broad and simplified. Keep the face readable and do not add a bow, quiver, small feathers, straps, or other fine equipment.
- **Gnoll Shaman:** Create one new standalone bitmap source icon for a GNOLL SHAMAN tabletop token. Give the gnoll a broad simplified bone crown or ritual headdress made from only a few massive connected shapes, plus one thick ritual staff rising behind one shoulder and fused into the main silhouette. The staff top should be a single large crescent or blunt fork shape with no tiny dangling charms. Keep the face readable and avoid necklaces, beads, runes, feathers, smoke, sparks, or other fine details.
- **Gnoll Pack Leader:** Create one new standalone bitmap source icon for a GNOLL PACK LEADER tabletop token. Make the gnoll older, broader, dominant, and visibly scarred using one large bold facial notch or stripe. Add a heavy trophy collar with only a few oversized blunt teeth and a broad crown-like bone ridge or compact headdress fused to the head. No weapon. The expression should be commanding. Avoid tiny trophies, chains, beads, scratches, narrow spikes, or other fine details.

The technical conversion only traces the artwork boundary. It does not use dilation, erosion, hole filling, island removal, or artwork thickening.
