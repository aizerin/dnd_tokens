# Monstrosities — Image Generation Prompts

Built-in ImageGen was used separately for every token.

## Shared prompt

> Use case: logo-brand. One original classic-fantasy monstrosity as a compact connected solid-black emblem for a flush two-colour 25 mm FDM token. Designed around 18 mm for a 0.4 mm nozzle and direct tracing without dilation, erosion, cleanup, hole filling, island removal or thickening. Every limb, bridge and opening is broad. Pure black on opaque white; no shading, colour, border, text, thin limbs, fine texture, tiny details or floating pieces.

## Role blocks

- **Mimic:** Monster treasure chest with two eyes, four blunt teeth and a fused tongue.
- **Owlbear:** Front-facing bear-and-owl head with hooked beak and broad feather-fur collar.
- **Basilisk:** Compact top-down reptile with eight short fused legs and curled tail.
- **Rust Monster:** Side-facing insectoid with four blocky legs, fused tail and looped antennae.
