# Mechanical Traps — Image generation prompts

Generated with the built-in ImageGen tool.

## Shared direction

Use case: logo-brand. Asset type: family-friendly classic-fantasy dungeon trap marker artwork for a flush two-colour 25 mm FDM token. Create one original non-graphic trap emblem matching a bold compact token collection as exactly one connected near-square solid-black silhouette with only large negative spaces. Use a clear top-down or front diagram-like view showing the activated trap. Designed around 18 mm for a 0.4 mm FDM nozzle and direct tracing without dilation, erosion, cleanup, hole filling, island removal, or line thickening. Every spike, blade, arrow, rope, bridge, and opening must be about 7% of artwork width or wider and fused to a thick trap frame. Pure black on opaque white with hard edges. No grayscale, transparency, shading, colour, token circle outside the trap, border, text, scenery, injury imagery, blood, tiny mechanisms, thin wire, narrow lines, isolated islands, floating pieces, people, or creatures.

## Creature requests

- **Dart Trap:** One front diagram of a thick wall frame with exactly three huge broad darts fused halfway out of three large openings, all joined by a broad base strip. No tiny darts.
- **Swinging Blade:** One front diagram of a huge broad crescent blade fused to a thick overhead pivot arm and a rectangular frame, with exactly three large openings. No chain.
- **Falling Net:** One top-down folded net represented by a thick square outer frame and exactly six broad diagonal bridges making exactly nine large openings. No thin mesh or rope ends.
- **Crushing Walls:** One top-down diagram of two huge opposing stone wall slabs fused by a thick outer frame, with one large narrowing central opening and exactly four broad motion-notch openings. No arrows.
