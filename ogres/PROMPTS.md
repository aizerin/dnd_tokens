# Ogres — Image Generation Prompts

The source artwork was created with the built-in image generator. Each token was generated separately with the shared prompt and one role block.

## Shared prompt

> Use case: logo-brand.
>
> Asset type: source artwork for a flush two-colour 25 mm FDM-printed tabletop token.
>
> Collection style: create one completely new original classic-fantasy OGRE pictogram matching a bold compact head-and-shoulders tabletop-token collection. The ogre must be unmistakably huge, crude and heavy through an extremely broad head, tiny low forehead, massive jaw, thick neck, small rounded ears and very wide shoulders. Use a strong graphic silhouette, readable monstrous face, role-defining headgear, and exactly one short piece of equipment integrated behind one shoulder. This is not an orc, goblin, troll, human, realistic portrait, line drawing, heraldic crest or comic mascot.
>
> Composition: exactly one front-facing ogre shown as a compact near-square head-and-shoulders emblem. Use one dominant solid-black silhouette. Keep equipment short, extremely thick, close to the shoulder, and firmly fused into the bust. Leave a generous white margin around the complete glyph.
>
> Face openings: exactly two large simple eye openings with no pupils, one very broad flat nose opening, and one thick neutral mouth opening. Include exactly two large blunt lower tusks as broad white negative shapes connected directly to the mouth opening. The mouth must remain closed and serious, with no other teeth. Add at most two other broad armour, hair or headgear gaps. Maximum seven white openings total.
>
> Manufacturing constraints: final glyph is approximately 18 mm wide on a 25 mm token and printed with a 0.4 mm FDM nozzle. It will be traced directly without dilation, erosion, cleanup, hole filling, island removal or line thickening. Every black bridge, equipment connector, tusk and white opening must be about 5% of the artwork width or wider. No thin scars, narrow weapon shafts, fragile spikes, tiny studs, individual hair strands, cloth folds, runes, isolated islands or floating pieces.
>
> Output: pure solid black artwork on a pure opaque white background with hard crisp edges. No grayscale, transparency, shading, gradients, texture, shadow, colour, token circle, rim, border, frame, text, letters or numbers. No blood, gore, severed parts, shield, animal, second character or unrequested equipment.

## Ogre Brute role block

> Primary request: OGRE BRUTE. Show a bald brutal ogre with one heavy sloping brow, a huge square jaw, and one broad torn leather shoulder collar. Add one short extremely thick wooden club behind one shoulder, fused into the head or collar, with a massive rounded end and no spikes. Use an angry but closed-mouth neutral expression. No helmet, hood, hair, beard, spear, cleaver, staff, axe, armour plates or decorative symbols.

## Ogre Hunter role block

> Primary request: OGRE HUNTER. Show a rugged ogre tracker with one single thick topknot shaped as a solid blunt loop fused into the skull, and one broad fur-like collar represented by only three large blunt outer points. Add one short extremely thick hunting spear behind one shoulder, fused into the topknot or collar, ending in one oversized broad leaf-shaped point. Use a watchful closed-mouth expression. No helmet, hood, beard, club, cleaver, bow, staff, animal trophy, bones or decorative symbols.

## Ogre Butcher role block

> Primary request: OGRE BUTCHER. Show a grim ogre camp cook and butcher wearing one low solid leather cap and one massive plain apron collar. Add one short extremely thick meat cleaver behind one shoulder, fused into the cap or collar, with one oversized rectangular blade, one broad blunt corner, and no hole in the blade. Use a stern closed-mouth expression. No blood, meat, helmet, hood, hair, beard, club, spear, staff, axe, kitchen utensils or decorative symbols.

## Ogre Shaman role block

> Primary request: OGRE SHAMAN. Show an old mysterious ogre wearing one oversized crooked hood with a single thick folded tip and one broad high robe collar. Give him one short heavy chin beard fused into the collar as one solid blunt mass. Add one short extremely thick ritual staff behind one shoulder, fused into the hood or collar, ending in one large solid crescent shape with no small holes or hanging pieces. Use a severe closed-mouth expression. No skull, bones, feathers, antlers, club, spear, cleaver, flame, spell effects, runes or decorative symbols.
