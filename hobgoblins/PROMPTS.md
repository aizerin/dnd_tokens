# Hobgoblin Artwork Prompts

All four images were created with the built-in image generator. The existing goblin and orc artwork was used only as a broad collection style reference.

## Hobgoblin Soldier

> Use case: logo-brand. Asset type: source artwork for a flush two-colour 25 mm FDM-printed tabletop token. Input images: Image 1 and Image 2 show the established small goblin faction; Image 3 shows the established heavier orc faction. They are style and family-scale references only. Create a completely new original Hobgoblin Soldier glyph. Do not copy a reference pose or equipment. Primary request: exactly one centered black-and-white pictogram of a disciplined Hobgoblin Soldier, compact front-facing head-and-shoulders emblem. Make him clearly distinct from both a small mischievous goblin and a broad tusked orc: taller-looking angular face, stern narrow eyes, medium pointed ears, no tusks, no grin. Give him a rigid segmented military helmet with broad cheek guards and one large rectangular shield fused tightly beside the shoulder. No spear, no bow, no crown. The armour and shield should communicate an organised professional army. Print constraints: final artwork approximately 18 mm wide on a 25 mm token, printed with a 0.4 mm FDM nozzle. There will be no later dilation, erosion, stroke thickening, or cleanup. Every black stroke, connector, facial feature, and important white gap must be broad, approximately 5% of the artwork width or more. Use thick rounded graphic shapes, large clear cutouts, generous padding, and a visually connected compact composition. Keep the shield connected with stout bridges. Color and background: pure solid black artwork on a pure white background, crisp hard edges. Avoid: enclosing circle, token rim, border, text, letters, numbers, gray, colour, gradients, shadows, texture, perspective, 3D rendering, fine linework, tiny holes, isolated floating fragments, extra characters, extra objects, tusks, axe, arrow, spear, and decorative micro-detail.

## Shared faction prompt for the remaining roles

> Use case: logo-brand. Asset type: source artwork for a flush two-colour 25 mm FDM-printed tabletop token. Input images: Image 1 is the newly established Hobgoblin Soldier and is the primary faction reference. Images 2 and 3 are broader collection style references only. Create a completely new original role glyph that clearly belongs to the same disciplined hobgoblin army as Image 1. Preserve the faction traits: tall-looking angular face, stern narrow eyes, medium pointed ears, no tusks, no grin, rigid segmented military armour, and a compact front-facing head-and-shoulders emblem. Do not copy Image 1's shield. Print constraints: final artwork approximately 18 mm wide on a 25 mm token, printed with a 0.4 mm FDM nozzle. There will be no later dilation, erosion, stroke thickening, or cleanup. Every black stroke, connector, facial feature, and important white gap must be broad, approximately 5% of the artwork width or more. Use thick rounded graphic shapes, large clear cutouts, generous padding, and a visually connected compact composition. Keep equipment connected with stout bridges. Color and background: pure solid black artwork on a pure white background, crisp hard edges. Avoid: enclosing circle, token rim, border, text, letters, numbers, gray, colour, gradients, shadows, texture, perspective, 3D rendering, fine linework, tiny holes, isolated floating fragments, extra characters, tusks, grin, decorative micro-detail, and equipment not requested below.

The following role block was appended to the shared prompt for each image:

- **Hobgoblin Spearman:** Use a rigid segmented helmet with a short narrow central crest and one oversized thick spear held vertically beside the shoulder, fused tightly into the bust. The broad spearhead must be immediately readable. No shield, no bow, no crown.
- **Hobgoblin Archer:** Use a lighter segmented military hood or close helmet and one oversized thick arrow crossing diagonally behind the head, fused into the bust. Make the arrow unmistakable at small size. No bow, no shield, no spear, no crown.
- **Hobgoblin Captain:** Use a commanding heavy helmet with broad cheek guards and one large transverse military crest across the top, plus a bold central rank badge on the collar. No crown, no shield, no weapon, no arrow, no staff. The helmet crest must dominate the silhouette.

The technical conversion only traces the artwork boundary. It does not use dilation, erosion, or artwork thickening.
