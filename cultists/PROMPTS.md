# Cultist Artwork Prompts

The original Koschei Cultist artwork predates this category expansion. The three new images were created with the built-in image generator, using the original token only as a faction and collection style reference.

## Shared faction prompt

> Use case: logo-brand. Asset type: source artwork for a flush two-colour 25 mm FDM-printed tabletop token. Input image: the supplied Koschei Cultist is the primary faction and collection style reference. Create a completely new original role glyph belonging to the same sinister masked cult. Preserve the faction language: compact front-facing hooded head-and-shoulders emblem, large simplified skull mask, dark robe, severe symmetry, and bold connected black-and-white shapes. Do not copy the crown emblem unless the requested role specifically needs rank.
>
> Manufacturing constraint: the artwork will be reduced to approximately 18 mm wide and converted directly to SVG for two-colour FDM printing with a 0.4 mm nozzle, without later dilation, erosion, cleanup, or line thickening. Use very thick connected shapes, very few large negative spaces, and generous clear gaps. Every essential black bridge, white gap, outline, mask opening, and role attribute must be about 5% of the artwork width or thicker. Avoid narrow necks, fragile spikes, tiny teeth, thin chains, small holes, thin slits, isolated islands, and floating pieces.
>
> Composition: exactly one centered compact near-square pictogram, visually balanced at token scale. Pure solid black artwork on a pure white background with crisp hard edges. No grayscale, shading, gradients, texture, shadows, colour, token border, enclosing circle, frame, lettering, caption, numbers, or text.

The following role block was appended to the shared prompt for each image:

- **Cultist Guard:** Primary request: CULTIST GUARD. Give the hooded skull-masked cultist a broad heavy rectangular ritual shield fused tightly beside one shoulder. The shield should have one single large vertical bone-like emblem or angular notch, with no tiny decoration. Add a heavier shoulder mantle to suggest armour. No spear, sword, staff, crown, magic, extra weapon, or micro-detail.
- **Cultist Mage:** Primary request: CULTIST MAGE. Give the hooded skull-masked cultist one thick ritual staff rising beside a shoulder and fused into the robe. The staff top must be a single large crescent surrounding one large solid orb connected by a stout bridge. Add one broad angular rune-like collar shape, but no readable symbol. No shield, sword, crown, flames, sparks, smoke, floating orb, tiny charms, or fine decoration.
- **Cult Leader:** Primary request: CULT LEADER. Make the hooded skull-masked figure broader and more commanding. Add a dominant bone headdress with exactly three large blunt connected prongs and a heavy high ceremonial collar with one oversized central diamond clasp. The headdress, hood, and collar must form one sturdy silhouette. No weapon, shield, staff, floating pieces, tiny jewellery, narrow horns, or decorative micro-detail.

The technical conversion only traces the artwork boundary. It does not use dilation, erosion, hole filling, island removal, or artwork thickening.
