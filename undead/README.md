# Undead

This category contains nine classic undead encounter roles and creatures.

## Tokens

1. Skeleton Warrior — damaged armour and chipped sword
2. Skeleton Archer — damaged armour and oversized arrow
3. Zombie — decayed asymmetric face and torn collar
4. Ghoul — feral skull-like face and raised claw
5. Ghost — hollow face and two spectral tails
6. Wight — broken helmet, heavy armour, and sword
7. Mummy — broad bandages and pharaonic collar
8. Vampire Spawn — two fangs and high cloak collar
9. Necromancer — hood, bone clasp, and skull-topped staff

## Printing

- 25 mm diameter;
- 1.0 mm total thickness;
- 0.4 mm colour inlay on each face;
- 0.2 mm central core;
- matching artwork on both faces, mirrored on the bottom;
- flush surfaces with no raised relief;
- base: extruder 2;
- artwork and outer ring: extruder 1;
- recommended layer height: 0.20 mm.

The file `3mf/undead-9-tokens.3mf` contains all nine tokens in a 3 × 3 layout.
