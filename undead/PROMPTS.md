# Undead Artwork Prompts

All nine images were created with the built-in image generator. The first Skeleton Warrior used existing collection artwork only as a broad visual reference. It then became the graphic-density reference for the remaining undead.

## Skeleton Warrior

> Use case: logo-brand. Asset type: source artwork for a flush two-colour 25 mm FDM-printed tabletop token. The input images are broad collection style references only: bold skull language, compact military bust, and undead character identity. Create one completely new original SKELETON WARRIOR pictogram.
>
> Subject: exactly one front-facing animated skeleton warrior, shown as a compact head-and-shoulders emblem. Use a large bare skull with two large eye sockets, one broad nasal opening, and only four oversized square teeth. Add simple damaged shoulder armour and one broad chipped sword silhouette fused diagonally behind one shoulder. No crown, hood, helmet, shield, bow, flesh, or second weapon. The skeleton must read immediately as a common low-level undead soldier rather than a king or necromancer.
>
> Manufacturing constraint: final artwork will be approximately 18 mm wide on a 25 mm token and printed with a 0.4 mm FDM nozzle. It will be converted directly to SVG with no later dilation, erosion, cleanup, or line thickening. Use only thick connected shapes and a few large negative spaces. Every essential black bridge, white gap, skull opening, tooth division, armour edge, and sword feature must be about 5% of the artwork width or thicker. Avoid thin bones, ribs, cracks, tiny teeth, narrow jaw hinges, fragile spikes, small holes, isolated islands, and floating fragments.
>
> Composition: centered, compact, near-square logo pictogram with a sturdy connected silhouette. Pure solid black artwork on a pure white background with hard crisp edges. No grayscale, antialias-style shading, gradients, texture, shadows, colour, token border, enclosing circle, frame, lettering, caption, numbers, or text.

## Shared prompt for the remaining creatures

> Use case: logo-brand. Asset type: source artwork for a flush two-colour 25 mm FDM-printed tabletop token. Input image: the supplied Skeleton Warrior is the primary collection style and graphic-density reference. Create a completely new original undead glyph, not a copy of its pose or equipment.
>
> Manufacturing constraint: final artwork will be approximately 18 mm wide on a 25 mm token and printed with a 0.4 mm FDM nozzle. It will be converted directly to SVG with no later dilation, erosion, cleanup, hole filling, island removal, or line thickening. Use only thick connected shapes and a few large negative spaces. Every essential black bridge, white gap, outline, facial opening, and identifying feature must be about 5% of the artwork width or thicker. Avoid thin bones, hair strands, ribs, cracks, stitches, tiny teeth, narrow jaw hinges, fragile spikes, small holes, isolated islands, and floating fragments.
>
> Composition: exactly one centered compact front-facing or slightly three-quarter head-and-shoulders emblem, near-square and sturdy at token scale. Pure solid black artwork on a pure white background with hard crisp edges. No grayscale, shading, gradients, texture, shadows, colour, token border, enclosing circle, frame, lettering, caption, numbers, or text.

The following role block was appended to the shared prompt for each image:

- **Skeleton Archer:** Preserve the same common bare-skull identity as the reference: two large eye sockets, one broad nasal opening, and exactly four oversized square teeth. Replace the sword with one oversized thick arrow crossing diagonally behind the head and fused into the shoulder silhouette. The broad arrowhead and fletching must be unmistakable. Use simple damaged shoulder armour. No bow, quiver, sword, shield, crown, hood, helmet, flesh, or second arrow.
- **Zombie:** Show one decayed human zombie head and torn shoulders, not a skeleton. Use an asymmetrical sagging face, one large round sunken eye and one narrow half-closed eye, a broad broken nose, and a heavy slack jaw with exactly three large blocky teeth. Include one single large missing chunk from the outer head silhouette and a torn collar. No exposed thin bones, no weapon, no skull mask, no small wounds, no fine stitches, no gore droplets, and no detached pieces.
- **Ghoul:** Show one hairless feral undead humanoid with a low brow, large hollow eyes, broad flattened nose, long heavy jaw, and exactly three oversized triangular teeth. Add two pointed but thick ears and one large claw silhouette raised beside the face and fused solidly into the shoulder. Make it clearly more predatory than the zombie. No weapon, armour, hair, ribs, tiny claws, saliva, tongue, blood, or floating parts.
- **Ghost:** Show one unmistakable spectral face with two large hollow eyes and one large open oval mouth, framed by a broad hoodless flowing shroud. The lower bust should taper into exactly two thick curling spectral tails that remain fused to the main silhouette. Keep all curves broad and simple. No skull, weapon, chains, hands, tiny wisps, separate flames, floating fragments, or detailed cloth folds.
- **Wight:** Show one ancient armoured undead warrior with a gaunt corpse face rather than a bare skull: deep large eye hollows, intact narrow nose ridge, sunken cheeks, and a closed severe mouth. Add a heavy broken helmet with one broad central ridge and a massive high armour collar. One large chipped sword hilt may rise behind a shoulder and must be fused into the silhouette. No crown, exposed teeth, hood, shield, thin spikes, or small decoration.
- **Mummy:** Show one front-facing ancient mummy head and shoulders wrapped in a small number of very broad bandages. Use exactly two large dark eye openings and one large dark mouth gap. The bandage strips must be thick, widely spaced, and fused into the head and shoulder silhouette; use no more than six major visible bands. Add one broad pharaonic shoulder collar shape without tiny ornament. No crown, staff, weapon, loose bandage ends, hieroglyphs, tiny tears, or floating pieces.
- **Vampire Spawn:** Show one predatory humanoid vampire head and shoulders, not a skeleton: swept-back solid hair silhouette, pointed but thick ears, two large angular eyes, a broad snarling mouth, and exactly two oversized upper fangs. Add a massive high cloak collar fused to both shoulders. Make it dangerous but not royal. No crown, jewellery, weapon, blood drops, tiny lower teeth, thin hair strands, cape folds, bats, or floating elements.
- **Necromancer:** Show one hooded living dark mage with a gaunt human face, two large shadowed eyes, and a severe closed mouth. Add one thick staff rising beside a shoulder and fused into the robe; its top must be one large simplified skull emblem with only two large eye holes and no tiny teeth. Add a broad bone-shaped collar clasp. No crown, skeletal face, exposed teeth, spell sparks, smoke, floating skulls, thin fingers, tiny runes, or fine robe decoration.

The technical conversion does not use dilation, erosion, hole filling, island removal, or artwork thickening. Trace sampling parameters were adjusted for three glyphs only to obtain clean manifold triangulation.
