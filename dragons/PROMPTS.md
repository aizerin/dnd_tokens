## Shared direction

Use case: logo-brand. Family-friendly classic-fantasy tabletop marker artwork for a flush two-colour 25 mm FDM token. Create one original Dragon Wyrmling head-and-shoulders emblem as exactly one connected near-square solid-black silhouette. Include two large eye openings, two broad nostril openings, one thick closed-mouth gap, and a distinctive large crest. Design around 18 mm for a 0.4 mm FDM nozzle and direct tracing without dilation, erosion, cleanup, hole filling, island removal, or line thickening. Every horn, crest, bridge, and opening must be about 5% of artwork width or wider. Use very few broad shapes. Pure black on opaque white with hard edges. No grayscale, transparency, shading, colour, token circle, border, text, wings, body, scenery, elemental effect, injury imagery, open mouth, teeth, thin spikes, tiny scales, narrow lines, isolated islands, floating pieces, or second creature.
