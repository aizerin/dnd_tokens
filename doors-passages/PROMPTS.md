# Doors & Passages — Image generation prompts

Generated with the built-in ImageGen tool.

## Shared direction

Use case: logo-brand. Asset type: family-friendly classic-fantasy dungeon object marker artwork for a flush two-colour 25 mm FDM token. Create one original object emblem matching a bold compact token collection as exactly one connected near-square solid-black silhouette with only large negative spaces. Use a clear front or slightly top-down view. Designed around 18 mm for a 0.4 mm FDM nozzle and direct tracing without dilation, erosion, cleanup, hole filling, island removal, or line thickening. Every handle, hinge, board, bridge, and opening must be about 7% of artwork width or wider and fused to the main silhouette. Pure black on opaque white with hard edges. No grayscale, transparency, shading, colour, token circle, border, text, scenery, tiny wood grain, thin chains, individual coins, narrow lines, isolated islands, floating pieces, people, or creatures.

## Creature requests

- **Wooden Door:** One closed arched wooden door in front view: thick outer frame fused to the door, exactly three broad plank openings and one large round handle opening. No wall.
- **Iron Door:** One closed heavy rectangular iron door in front view: thick outer frame, exactly four broad inset panels and one huge central wheel handle fused to the door with one large opening.
- **Secret Door:** One square stone wall panel in front view: thick cracked outline represented by exactly four broad zigzag gaps and one subtle large eye-shaped central opening. No text or arrows.
- **Trapdoor:** One square floor hatch in top-down view: thick outer frame, exactly four broad plank openings and one huge ring handle fused to the hatch with one large central opening.
