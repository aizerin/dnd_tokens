# Troglodytes — Image generation prompts

Generated with the built-in ImageGen tool.

## Shared direction

Use case: logo-brand. Asset type: family-friendly classic-fantasy tabletop game marker artwork for a flush two-colour 25 mm FDM token. Create one original Troglodyte emblem as exactly one front-facing reptilian cave humanoid head-and-shoulders figure in a connected near-square solid-black silhouette, with a broad lizard snout, two large eye openings, thick neck, and only large negative spaces. Designed around 18 mm for a 0.4 mm FDM nozzle and direct tracing without morphology or cleanup. Every feature must be about 5% of artwork width or wider and connected. Pure black on opaque white with hard edges. No grayscale, transparency, shading, colour, token circle, border, text, scenery, injury imagery, tiny scales, thin spikes, narrow lines, isolated islands, floating pieces, or second creature.

## Creature requests

- **Troglodyte Hunter:** Low backward head crest, one thick short spear fused diagonally behind a shoulder, and exactly three broad jaw ridges. Closed mouth, no teeth.
- **Troglodyte Warrior:** Tall blunt central crest, two broad cheek plates, and one short thick stone axe fused behind one shoulder. Closed mouth.
- **Troglodyte Brute:** Huge square snout, heavy brow, exactly four broad neck spines fused into the shoulders, and no weapon. Closed mouth.
- **Troglodyte Shaman:** Rounded hood of broad cave frills, one thick fork-topped staff fused to a shoulder, exactly one large opening in the staff top, and no spell effects.
