# Bards — Image generation prompts

Generated with the built-in ImageGen tool.

## Shared direction

Use case: logo-brand. Asset type: family-friendly classic-fantasy adventurer or NPC marker artwork for a flush two-colour 25 mm FDM token. Create one original humanoid class emblem matching a bold compact token collection as exactly one front-facing head-and-shoulders figure in one connected near-square solid-black silhouette, with two large eye openings and only large negative spaces. Designed around 18 mm for a 0.4 mm FDM nozzle and direct tracing without dilation, erosion, cleanup, hole filling, island removal, or line thickening. Every weapon, instrument, fur lock, bridge, and opening must be about 6% of artwork width or wider and fused to the main silhouette. Pure black on opaque white with hard edges. No grayscale, transparency, shading, colour, token circle, border, text, scenery, injury imagery, tiny jewellery, thin hair, thin strings, narrow blades, isolated islands, floating pieces, spell particles, or second character.

## Creature requests

- **Minstrel:** Cheerful hoodless performer with a broad feathered cap where the feather reconnects to the brim, high collar and one compact lute fused to a shoulder with exactly one large sound opening. No strings.
- **Skald:** Armored northern singer with a low helmet, broad braided beard represented by exactly three sections and one thick horn-shaped instrument fused to a shoulder with one large opening.
- **Court Bard:** Elegant performer with a broad curled hat, high ceremonial collar and one compact harp fused to a shoulder, represented by exactly three large openings and no thin strings.
- **Battle Bard:** Armored performer with a broad plume helmet, one short thick sword fused behind one shoulder and one compact drum fused behind the other with one large central opening.
