# Objects and Traps Production Plan

This production run adds 40 utility tokens in 10 category folders.

## Objects and dungeon features

- Containers & Loot: Treasure Chest, Locked Chest, Barrel, Crate
- Doors & Passages: Wooden Door, Iron Door, Secret Door, Trapdoor
- Dungeon Features: Altar, Statue, Fountain, Well
- Camp & Supplies: Campfire, Tent, Bedroll, Supply Cart
- Magic & Objectives: Magic Portal, Ritual Circle, Crystal Orb, Treasure Pile
- Cover & Obstacles: Table, Bookshelf, Barricade, Rubble Pile

## Traps and hazards

- Floor Traps: Pit Trap, Spike Pit, Pressure Plate, Collapsing Floor
- Mechanical Traps: Dart Trap, Swinging Blade, Falling Net, Crushing Walls
- Magical Traps: Fire Rune, Frost Rune, Lightning Rune, Binding Rune
- Environmental Hazards: Poison Gas, Rolling Boulder, Cave-In, Quicksand

All categories use English names, direct source tracing without morphology, 25 mm diameter, 1.0 mm total thickness, 0.4 mm colour inlay on both faces, a 0.2 mm centre core, base on extruder 2, and artwork plus perimeter ring on extruder 1.
